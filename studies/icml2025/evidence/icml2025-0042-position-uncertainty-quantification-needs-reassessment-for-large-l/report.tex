\# 3. Problem 1: the Wimmer axioms do not imply near-zero EU\par
Source claim and location. Section 2.1 and Figure 2, p3, use the equally weighted mixture of Bernoulli parameters zero and one as the first central contrast: disagreement/MI is maximal, while the cited Wimmer axiomatic view is asserted to yield approximately zero EU. The proposed explanation is that only two models remain possible. The questionable step is treating this intuition as a consequence of the cited axioms.\par
Primary-source check. Wimmer et al., UAI 2023, printed p2285 (PDF p4), A2 makes the uniform distribution on the simplex a global EU maximum. A3 requires a mean-preserving spread not to decrease EU, with a strict alternative also stated. Footnote 3 defines the spread through conditional mean-zero noise. A1 applies to a single Dirac measure; an endpoint mixture is not a single Dirac measure.\par
\[U\sim\operatorname{Unif}[0,1],\quad B\mid U=u\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(u),\quad Q_B=\tfrac12\delta_0+\tfrac12\delta_1.\qquad \mathbb E[B-U\mid U=u]=0.\] Complete assumption check. Both distributions are valid distributions over the binary probability simplex, represented by the scalar Bernoulli parameter. Integrating P(B=1|U=u)=u gives P(B=1)=1/2. Both means are 1/2. Setting Z=B-U supplies exactly the conditional mean-zero noise required by the source definition, so Q\_B is a mean-preserving spread of Q\_U, with no approximation or limit involved.\par
\[\mathrm{EU}(Q_B)\geq\mathrm{EU}(Q_U)\geq\mathrm{EU}(Q_B),\qquad \mathrm{EU}(Q_B)=\mathrm{EU}(Q_U)=\max_Q\mathrm{EU}(Q).\] Under the strict A3 reading, this nontrivial spread instead exposes incompatibility with A2 on the same pair. Neither reading gives the nontrivial near-zero value asserted in the reviewed paper. This argument does not claim to construct a functional satisfying an inconsistent full axiom set, and does not use inconsistency to infer arbitrary conclusions.\par
Independent finite/numerical checks confirm MI(Q\_B)=1 bit and MI(Q\_U)=1-1/(2 ln 2), approximately 0.27865248 bits; variances of the parameter distributions are 1/4 and 1/12. These checks illustrate the example; the conditional coupling above is the decisive proof.\par
Alternative reading and counterevidence. Wimmer’s p2286 discussion does support the intuition that knowing the parameter is an endpoint can convey more knowledge than a uniform distribution does. The philosophical tension about knowledge and disagreement is real. It does not establish the reviewed paper’s stronger zero-versus-maximum claim as an axiomatic necessity.\par
Impact and correction scope. This invalidates the first central technical demonstration used to motivate reassessment, warranting Major. It leaves open independent conceptual disagreements and all empirical questions about useful uncertainty for agents. A defensible revision must remove the purported axiomatic near-zero implication or provide an explicitly specified measure with verified assumptions. No replacement axiom system is claimed to have been repaired or validated. Evidence: independent\_math\_memo.json, independent\_math\_checks.py/results.json, and the archived Wimmer PDF and p2285 image.\par
\# 4. Problem 2: a true-parameter consistency requirement is misapplied to model bias\par
Source claim and location. Section 2.2, p4, contrasts a restricted model class with a richer data-generating mechanism. It then invokes Bengs et al.’s infinite-sample requirement as a corollary forcing remaining model bias out of EU and into AU. Figure 3 and the theoretical framing make this an evidentiary step in the second principal example, not just an unrelated terminology aside.\par
Actual cited requirement. Bengs et al., NeurIPS 2022, p8, Section 4, Definition 1(A2), considers iid categorical observations with true probability parameter theta*. It requires the learned second-order distribution Q\_N to converge in probability to the Dirac measure at theta*. The original simplex contains the true parameter. This is a consistency requirement used to assess second-order loss functions, not a universal theorem that reclassifies arbitrary model misspecification.\par
\[Y_i\overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}\operatorname{Bernoulli}(0.8),\quad \Theta_{\mathcal H}=\{0.5\}\ \Longrightarrow\ Q_N=\delta_{0.5}\ \forall N.\qquad \int\theta\,dQ_N=0.5\ne0.8=\int\theta\,d\delta_{0.8}.\] This is an applicability check, not a counterexample purporting to satisfy Bengs A2. The fixed test-function gap proves the restricted sequence cannot converge to the required true-parameter target. Moving the target to the best approximation would define a different requirement and must be stated. Failure to express the truth does not itself imply that residual model bias must be labeled AU.\par
Counterevidence and faithful terminology. Hullermeier and Waegeman do call the risk minimizer within a hypothesis class a Bayes predictor; that phrase alone is not an error. Their discussion distinguishes it from the pointwise Bayes predictor, groups model and approximation uncertainty with EU, and assumes correct specification when setting model uncertainty aside. Schweighofer et al., arXiv:2410.10786v1, pp2-3, genuinely offer an alternative selected-model entropy definition of AU. The reviewed quotation is accurate. That source also retains a selected-versus-true KL term, and its setup states representability of the true predictive distribution.\par
Impact. Legitimate differences between uncertainty conventions therefore survive. What fails is the specific argument that the cited consistency axiom forces misspecification bias to be AU. The inference is central to the paper’s second theoretical example and thus supports Major under the task’s core-derivation standard. This judgment does not assert that all of Section 2.2, Table 1, or the proposed agent agenda is false.\par
Scoped correction. State the true-parameter target and its expressibility requirement; remove the forced-AU corollary when truth is excluded. Separately name true-distribution entropy, selected-model entropy, estimation uncertainty and model discrepancy. This is a supported correction of the inference, not a verified replacement theory of all uncertainty categories. Evidence and exact historical versions are recorded in source\_audit\_memo.json and its two acquisition manifests.\par
\# 5. Problem 3: Eq. (2) omits a context condition\par
Source claim and location. Section 3.1, p6, Eq. (2), expresses output prediction as a mixture over a finite or continuous task variable t, but its within-task factor is P(y|t) while task weights depend on input context x.\par
\[P(y\mid x)=\int P(y\mid t,x)P(t\mid x)\,dt.\qquad P(y\mid t,x)=P(y\mid t)\ \text{requires}\ Y\perp X\mid T.\] Admissible counterexample. Use the explicitly permitted finite task space with one task T=t0. Let X be equiprobable on two distinct literal contexts 0 and 1, and let the literal output Y equal X deterministically. These are valid normalized probabilities; no task uncertainty is needed. At X=1 the true left-hand side P(Y=1|X=1)=1. The printed mixture has one unit-weight term P(Y=1|T=t0)=1/2. The corrected factor P(Y=1|T=t0,X=1)=1 restores equality.\par
A charitable interpretation is that context is suppressed in notation or that the task variable includes every relevant context feature. Neither condition is stated in the displayed formula. The surrounding text already recognizes that context can determine the output. Because no algorithm, experiment or quantitative guarantee is shown to rely on the omission, the supported impact is Minor, a local formalization defect. The conditional-probability repair is analytically verified; adoption is pending.\par
\# 6. Scope clarifications and evidence that survives\par
Section 2.3, p5. Eq. (1) is an exact entropy identity. High correlation of component values across inputs cannot invalidate the identity. Conversely, an exact identity does not establish identifiable or operationally useful AU/EU semantics. The most faithful reading of the surrounding discussion is concern about meaningful disentanglement by practical estimators. A universal impossibility claim should be narrowed accordingly.\par
\[\mathrm{AU}=q,\quad \mathrm{EU}=h(q/2)-q,\quad\mathrm{TU}=h(q/2),\quad 0<q<0.4;\qquad \mathrm{EU}'(q)=\tfrac12\log_2\!\frac{2-q}{q}-1>0.\] The reviewer calculation independently sums the joint distribution for mutual information. At q=0.1,0.2,0.3, the maximum entropy-identity residual is 1.94e-16. This example establishes the logical distinction; it is not an empirical rebuttal of Mucsanyi et al. Their NeurIPS 2024 study explicitly allows some true-component correlation and reports a dataset-specific gap between it and correlations in assessed estimators. Its empirical concern remains relevant.\par
Gruber et al.’s cited 2023 v1 distinguishes nonadditive prediction-interval width (p4 Eq. (4)) from exact additive mean-square-error contributions (pp4-5 Eq. (5)). The distinction already exists in that historical version. Nonadditivity on one scale is not evidence of impossibility on every scale. No separate Major is assigned to this scope clarification.\par
Section 2.4, p6, concerns changing contexts and decisions about clarification. The text acknowledges that input points can change within a fixed string space. This is not a demonstrated contradiction of conditional probability. Sections 3-4 propose research directions and retain roles for numerical uncertainty, active learning and existing categories; no blanket claim that all conventional uncertainty methods are useless is attributed to the paper.\par
Empirical citation checks. CLAMBER (ACL 2024), Table 3, gives 57.38\% ambiguity-detection accuracy for ChatGPT under the specified zero-shot chain-of-thought setting; Table 7 gives 53.29\% helpfulness for a different few-shot chain-of-thought setting. The approximate 57\% and 53\% motivation is supported, with distinct prompting conditions. AmbigQA (EMNLP 2020), Table 2, reports 44\% single-answer questions among 2,004 test questions, hence 56\% with multiple answers. These are benchmark- and setting-specific results, not estimates for every current LLM or every real-world query.\par
The paper’s bibliometric illustration was inspected as motivation, but its arXiv query/count pipeline was not rerun. No missing experiment or code is itself treated as an error in the normative recommendations.\par

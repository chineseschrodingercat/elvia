\# 2. Central method: assumptions and checks\par
The paper pretrains an atom-based conditional GFlowNet using inexpensive property rewards and an offline likelihood term, then adapts it to additional rewards using TB or RTB. For Eq. (1), positivity of terminal rewards, complete trajectories, and the specified forward/backward distributions are required for the logarithmic trajectory-balance identity. The condition-dependent normalizer is implicit in the concise notation. The inspected implementation sums step log probabilities.\par
For Eq. (2), the experimental preference values are d in \{-1,0,1\}, the decay parameter is positive, and the lower bound is strictly below the upper bound. The three pieces agree at the interval boundaries and remain positive. The broad textual allowance of arbitrary real d is not needed by the stated experiments. Products in Eqs. (3) and (5) preserve positivity under these operative assumptions.\par
For Eq. (6), the pretrained trajectory distribution is fixed. Summing a satisfied RTB identity over all trajectories to a molecule yields exactly the stated posterior proportional to pretrained terminal mass times the fine-tuning reward. This remains true with multiple paths to one molecule. Retaining property rewards during adaptation is explicitly part of that target, so it is not an independently demonstrated double-counting error. This algebra does not establish optimizer convergence or exact reward sampling after mixed MLE/TB training.\par
\[Z_\phi P_\phi(\tau\mid c)=R_{\rm ft}(x\mid c)P_\theta(\tau\mid c)\ \Longrightarrow\ Z_\phi p_\phi(x\mid c)=R_{\rm ft}(x\mid c)p_\theta(x\mid c).\]\par
\# 3. Problem 1: metric definitions and reported values disagree\par
Location: Table 2 and Table 3 (p6), Appendix E (p22), and Table 10 (p26). Appendix E divides both the number of unique novel molecules and the number of unique molecules by the total generated count. Novelty must therefore be at most uniqueness. FragmentGFN is reported with novelty 0.97 and uniqueness 0.001 in Table 2. The gap cannot be explained by displayed rounding. Using novel-among-unique as a different denominator could explain it, but that is not the published definition. The AGFN row is not used as an independent contradiction because the displayed novelty 1.0 may be coarsely rounded.\par
Section 6 defines modes using reward at least 0.5. For a common nonnegative reward pool containing at least 100 samples, ten modes imply at least ten rewards of 0.5 or above, and hence a top-100 mean at least 0.05. The scratch logP row of Table 3 reports ten modes together with top-100 reward below 0.00001. The released get\_metrics uses the same sample/reward arrays for these quantities. Appendix Table 10 instead reports zero modes for that scratch logP row, which is concrete evidence of a local table discrepancy rather than proof of a failed GFlowNet.\par
The stated reward-weighted diversity is top-100 reward multiplied by a diversity in [0,1]. Table 10’s scratch MW row gives top-100 reward 0.00001 but RWTD 0.12. A reward-weighted count over 1,000 generated molecules cannot exceed 0.01 at that reward, whereas RW\#C is 20.14. The analogous main-table quantity is labeled raw diversity; current get\_metrics returns raw diversity/counts and top-K rewards separately. Mislabeling or a missing table-assembly transformation is plausible. No authenticated table-generation script establishes which explanation is correct.\par
Judgment: Minor reporting inconsistency. These contradictions damage interpretation of the affected statistics, but do not by themselves prove that AGFN’s broad advantage disappears. Repair verification: exact inequalities and independent arithmetic establish the inconsistency, not the correct historical replacement values. Required evidence is the raw generated sets, reward arrays, canonicalization rules, metric definitions actually used, and table-assembly script. Do not substitute a new denominator or zero modes without checking those records.\par

\# 5. Problem 3: released sampler can misalign molecules and SMILES\par
Location: src/agfn/sampling.py, lines 184-199, especially the deduplication and subsequent zip; downstream src/agfn/metrics.py. After remove\_duplicates shortens the SMILES list, the molecular-object list is filtered by membership in that shortened list. Membership retains repeated objects with the same string, rather than applying the returned retained indices. The two lists can consequently have different lengths and corresponding entries.\par
Exact finite witness: a valid batch with strings [CC, CC, CCC] and molecular objects [ethane\_1, ethane\_2, propane] produces unique strings [CC, CCC], while membership filtering retains all three objects. The subsequent zip emits (CC, ethane\_1) and (CCC, ethane\_2). All molecules are valid, the duplicate is permitted by sampling, and the fault is within the deduplication branch used by default. No stochastic/model execution is required. The independent replay uses plain indices and strings rather than importing author code.\par
This mismatch matters because the metrics path computes rewards from returned molecular objects and structural quantities from returned strings. A propane string can thus be paired with an ethane-derived property reward. A batch with no duplicates does not trigger this witness. Applying the same retained indices to both arrays is the appropriate local correction; the complete corrected author pipeline was not executed or modified.\par
Judgment: Minor demonstrated release defect; effect on published experiments Needs Evidence. The current sampler resamples until the target number of distinct strings is reached, whereas several published tables report uniqueness below one. This is specific counterevidence against assuming that this exact sampler produced those tables. The current commit’s date alone does not supply the missing run linkage.\par
\# 6. Additional implementation correspondence: bounded unresolved effects\par
The released success\_percentage counts molecules satisfying every property simultaneously and extends the d=0 interval by 10\%. The main-text definition averages per-property indicators and uses the stated interval. These are different estimands: with two properties and two molecules, each satisfying only one property and failing the other well outside the tolerance, the paper’s mean is 50\% while joint success is 0\%. The released L1 routine uses distance to an interval or preference endpoint, whereas the main text describes a 10th-percentile reference. These are confirmed current-code/documentation differences; the values actually used in historical tables require logs and a pinned evaluation entry point.\par
The docking helper returns both hit fractions normalized by a deduplicated/filtered set and alternatives normalized by the original sample count. No active caller selecting the published table fields was identified. It would therefore be unsupported to assert that the inflated denominator convention generated the paper’s numbers.\par
The unique-filtered binding rows outperform the unfiltered rows substantially, but deduplication alone does not demonstrate an unfair comparison. The primary Saturn paper specifies a 3,000-oracle-call budget and canonical-SMILES caching; repeats need not consume oracle calls. Matching AGFN’s total adaptation/evaluation oracle budget, filtering, docking repeats, and run aggregation is still Needs Evidence. No unequal-budget performance reversal is claimed here.\par
\# 7. Formula clarifications excluded from substantive findings\par
Equation (4), p4, prints the MLE term as a product of log probabilities, rather than the negative sum of step log probabilities. For a two-step path with both probabilities changing from 1/2 to 1/4, the printed quantity decreases from -0.48045 to -1.92181 despite a less likely path. However, tb\_loss.py:107 sums step logs and line111 negates the offline path values, matching ordinary MLE. The isolated printed operator is recorded as a notation clarification under the task’s scope, not as evidence that training used the wrong objective.\par
Appendix E, p22, includes diagonal pairs in its printed diversity sum. This would subtract an additional 2/(N-1), including an impossible negative value for identical molecules. The checked implementations at metrics\_utils.py:449-466 exclude diagonal pairs, including the active fast path. This provides direct counterevidence to a claim that the reported diversity computation necessarily used the erroneous formula. Neither formula typo is counted as a substantive Major or Minor finding.\par

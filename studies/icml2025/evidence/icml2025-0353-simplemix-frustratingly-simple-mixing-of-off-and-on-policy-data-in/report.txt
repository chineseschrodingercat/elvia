# Checked objective and empirical comparisons

Equation (1) maximizes the expected DPO log-sigmoid objective. With \(z=(x,y_w,y_l)\) and loss \(\ell_\theta(z)\), sampling a whole pair from the two sources with probability \(1/2\) gives \[\mathbb E_{z\sim (P_{\rm on}+P_{\rm off})/2}\ell_\theta(z)
 =\tfrac12\mathbb E_{P_{\rm on}}\ell_\theta(z)+\tfrac12\mathbb E_{P_{\rm off}}\ell_\theta(z).\] This is an identity for the objective, not a claim that fitted policies or test scores average linearly. Section 4.1 fixes the number of training pairs and one epoch; it explicitly acknowledges unequal generation cost. Its on-policy definition includes initial-SFT samples; footnote 1 acknowledges its difference from strict current-policy RL terminology. Neither is treated as an undisclosed error.

Independent decimal arithmetic confirms that SIMPLEMIX exceeds all four baseline LC entries in every model–dataset cell of Table 2. Recomputing the eight-task SIMPLEMIX means from Tables 4–5 gives \(63.0575\), \(62.12375\), \(61.13625\), and \(61.22125\) for Llama–UltraFeedback, Llama–HelpSteer2, Tulu–UltraFeedback, and Tulu–HelpSteer2. These round to the reported scores. Individual tasks do not all improve, but the main claim is about aggregate metrics.

# Clarification: denominator of the abstract gains

The abstract on p. 1 states average improvements of \(6.03\%\) over on/off-policy DPO and \(3.05\%\) over HyPO/DPO-Mix-P, without naming a model subset. For each cell \(c\), let \(s_c\) be SIMPLEMIX LC in percentage units and \(b_{cj}\) one of the two relevant baselines. Define the equally weighted percentage-point difference \[\Delta(C)=\frac{1}{2|C|}\sum_{c\in C}\sum_{j=1}^2(s_c-b_{cj}).\] Table 2 supplies all operands. The actual independent script yields:

Model scope | Versus on/off DPO | Versus hybrids

Tulu, both datasets | 6.025 | 3.050

Llama, both datasets | 3.0025 | 1.775

Both models, both datasets | 4.51375 | 2.4125

Thus the printed headline values are reproducible after rounding under a Tulu-only interpretation. This admissible reading is decisive counterevidence against declaring them fabricated or numerically false. They are not the pooled two-model gains, and they are absolute percentage-point differences rather than relative percentage increases. The clarification needed is to state the aggregation population and unit. Specifying “Tulu across both datasets” preserves the headline numbers; reporting the equally weighted full grid instead gives \(4.51\) and \(2.41\) percentage points. The calculation verifies these two alternatives, not which one the authors intended. No author correction was available in the frozen source set.

# Supporting checks

Figure 7 and Appendix B imply approximately 35 math and 57 coding prompts among 805 judged examples, compared with 122 creative-writing and 146 recommendation prompts. Figure 2’s subgroup directions and Figure 8’s mixed-data tradeoffs are visible, but paired judgments, category assignments, LC fitting details and training seeds were not available. Treating the LC values as independent binomial win counts would be unjustified; no such significance test was performed. Figure 3’s bootstrapped bars do not by themselves establish a causal task-type mechanism. The paper explicitly limits generalization in Section 7, which should accompany its broader verbal conclusions.

Figures 4–6 show the reported mixture and filtering trends, including overlapping uncertainty bands. The selected half-and-half ratio is the largest plotted point estimate, not a verified uniformly optimal ratio. Appendix A specifies a hyperparameter sweep but does not supply its selection traces. Appendix D specifies greedy evaluation and a 2048-token cap. Figures 10–11 support the reported reward-distribution shift under the same reward model; they are not independent human-quality measurements. None of these limitations alone proves the original results wrong.

The frozen source preparation did not identify an associated author repository after bounded title/link checks. Generic OpenRLHF and evaluation-harness links do not establish the authors’ exact executable version. No author program was run, and historical training, benchmark contamination, paired uncertainty and later revisions remain unverified.

No substantive objective error or aggregate-ranking contradiction was found within the inspected scope. The headline denominator and empirical uncertainty remain open.

\# Checked distribution and feasibility reasoning\par
Let \(p(a\mid h)\) be a normalized base policy at a prefix \(h\), already zero on infeasible actions, and let \(A\) be the union of parent tokens (parent edges in routing). Define \(Z=\sum_{a\in A}p(a\mid h)\). When \(Z>0\), Eq. (2)’s normalized restriction is \(p_A(a\mid h)=\mathbf1_A(a)p(a\mid h)/Z\). Averaging over the Bernoulli mutation in Eq. (3) gives \[q(a\mid h)=\begin{cases}
\mu p(a\mid h)+(1-\mu)p_A(a\mid h),&Z>0,\\
p(a\mid h),&Z=0.
\end{cases}\] Both branches are normalized and preserve the base policy’s zero probabilities for infeasible actions. Equation (3) can also be read as the distribution conditional on a realized mutation draw; the above expression simply marginalizes that auxiliary randomness. It is not an inconsistency between the two readings. For \(p=(0.2,0.3,0.5)\), \(A=\{1,2\}\) and \(\mu=0.1\), the restriction is \((0.4,0.6,0)\) and the marginal is \((0.38,0.57,0.05)\). When \(Z=0\), one must use the stated fallback without evaluating an undefined normalized restriction.\par
For the complete-graph TSP in Section 3.1.1, assume the base policy assigns positive mass to at least one unvisited city whenever any remain. Selecting only unvisited cities and finally returning to the start produces a Hamiltonian cycle. The parent restriction cannot invalidate that induction because the forced mutation restores the original feasible support whenever necessary. The independent script enumerates a six-city example with two explicit parent tours, positive nonuniform base weights and \(\mu\in\{0,0.01,1\}\). It checks all reachable prefixes, verifies transition normalization and sums complete-tour probability. All three totals are one up to floating-point error; forced fallback states occur. This finite check illustrates the general support argument and does not establish optimality or universal constraint satisfaction for every application.\par
The implementation in :232–248 intersects the current feasibility mask with the parent edge mask, tests empty intersections, and restores the original mask on forced or stochastic mutation. Parent masks are formed as unions of the selected tours’ edges, with both directions included (lines 422–451 and 486–520), appropriate for the symmetric routing setting. The script did not import or execute this file.\par
\# Selection, experiments and limitations\par
For positive \(\kappa\), Eq. (5)’s weights \(w_j=(\kappa|P|+j)^{-1}\) are finite, positive and decreasing in rank \(j\). The reported routing value \(\kappa=0.001\) satisfies that condition. Parent selection without replacement requires at least two individuals; the reported population size is 100. The finite four-rank calculation confirms normalization and decreasing weights. The source uses rank inverses and samples without replacement (lines 402–413, 477–490). Its duplicate-handling and novelty extensions were read but not exhaustively verified.\par
Stochastic replacement need not preserve the best individual in the working population. This does not refute a deterministic-elitism theorem, because none is claimed. The routing implementation separately retains the best encountered result in lines 321–334. Moreover, the paper explicitly disclaims guarantees for generated distributions and convergence (p. 9), and notes dependence on the base policy’s support. Local search after generation is disclosed in Appendix B.1 for the routing comparisons, so the reported results should not be interpreted as gains without that postprocessing.\par
Figures 2–4 were actually viewed: the algorithm schematic and forced-mutation illustration match the analyzed rule, and the four panels of Figure 4 identify three minimization objectives and one maximization objective. Their plotted ordering is an observation of the supplied figure, not an independently measured benchmark result. Appendix E.1 explicitly reports an exception on CVRP1000, which limits any universal superiority reading. Appendix D also acknowledges increased sequential generation calls and routing edge-matrix memory. The 8K training plus 2K search molecular budget is disclosed in Sections 5.3 and Appendix B.3.\par
The reported means, standard deviations, toxicity/diversity measures, molecular validity and oracle evaluation counts were not recomputed. The language and molecular code paths, third-party solvers, weights, datasets, and omitted result files were not independently validated. The source-preparation snapshot excluded such large or non-source assets; the saved OpenReview metadata attempt returned 403. No later erratum or external discussion was established. These limits prevent an inference of whole-paper correctness from the present checks.\par

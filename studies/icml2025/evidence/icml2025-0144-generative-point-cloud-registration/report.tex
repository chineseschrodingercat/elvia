\# Checked reasoning and assumptions\par
\#\# Registration and coupled attention\par
Equation (1), p. 3, defines a rigid alignment objective with \(R\in SO(3)\), \(t\in\mathbb R^3\), and ground-truth correspondences used to state the target problem. The text explicitly acknowledges that these correspondences are unavailable at deployment; this equation is not evidence of ground-truth correspondence leakage in the claimed algorithm.\par
The method projects depth-camera point clouds through known camera intrinsics and stacks the source and target depth maps and noisy latents vertically (Section 3.2–3.3, pp. 3–4). If each latent contains \(H'W'\) tokens in \(\mathbb R^d\), the combined representation contains \(2H'W'\) tokens. Equation (4) then has a \((2H'W')\times(2H'W')\) attention matrix, allowing cross-view terms. This verifies the claimed capacity for feature interaction. It is not a mathematical proof that arbitrary stochastic generations preserve exact correspondence colors. Section 3.4, p. 5, expressly recognizes remaining geometric and texture inconsistencies and motivates fine-tuning; that admission is relevant counterevidence against reading the architecture discussion as a universal consistency theorem.\par
The training description selects 3,000 pairs from the ScanNet training split, while the evaluation describes official dataset splits and wider frame spacing (Section 4.1–4.2, pp. 5–7). No contradiction establishing train–test overlap was found in that description. Pair identifiers and training logs were unavailable, so independence of the actual splits was not independently reconstructed.\par
\#\# Feature fusion\par
Equation (6), p. 5, concatenates weighted geometric and compressed image descriptors. For comparable descriptors \(g,c\) and \(g',c'\), the resulting squared Euclidean distance is \[\|[\omega g;(1-\omega)c]-[\omega g';(1-\omega)c']\|_2^2
 =\omega^2\|g-g'\|_2^2+(1-\omega)^2\|c-c'\|_2^2 .\] The squares follow from concatenation and do not constitute an error: the paper specifies feature weights, not an unsquared linear mixture of distances.\par
A shared PCA transform and common feature normalization would make corresponding image descriptors comparable. The prose does not establish precisely how the PCA basis is fitted and shared in the released experiment pipeline; the staged repository cannot resolve this. Independent per-cloud PCA fitting could cause a coordinate-basis mismatch, but the paper does not require such a fitting procedure. It is therefore an unresolved implementation detail, not a demonstrated defect.\par
\# Published comparisons and a rejected candidate concern\par
Tables 1–4 and their associated ablation descriptions were inspected. Changes across rotation accuracy, translation accuracy, error means, and medians should not be read as improvement in every column for every variant. In particular, the ColorPCR block of Table 3, PDF p. 8, contains the following changes:\par
Metric | Baseline | Generative | Signed benefit\par
Median rotation error (degrees) | 1.8 | 1.9 | \(-0.1\)\par
Translation accuracy at 5 cm (\%) | 48.3 | 47.3 | \(-1.0\)\par
Median translation error (cm) | 5.2 | 5.3 | \(-0.1\)\par
Rotation accuracy at 5 degrees (\%) | 79.9 | 83.6 | \(+3.7\)\par
Mean translation error (cm) | 41.8 | 28.3 | \(+13.5\)\par
For errors, signed benefit means baseline minus generative; for accuracies, it means generative minus baseline. Independent exact-decimal calculations confirm these values.\par
In extracted plain text, the row labeled improvement contains positive magnitudes for the first three changes. The original PDF, however, shows those three magnitudes in red, and improved quantities in cyan; the best baseline entries are also bolded. Thus the actual visual evidence distinguishes the deteriorations. No signed-calculation Problem is assigned on the basis of the text extraction. The broad comparative claim is supported by many improved aggregate entries in the published table, while the displayed adverse entries limit any stronger all-metrics reading. This establishes internal reporting consistency only, not independent reproduction or statistical significance of the gains.\par

Appendix C contains incorrect flattened-index equivalences: a missing absolute value and a temporal offset formulation that fails at both signs and frame boundaries. These are repairable local mathematical errors. The valid permutation argument and the inspected later implementation do not support concluding that the reported generation gains are invalid. Benchmark reproduction and historical kernel correspondence remain unverified.

# P1: flattened-index equivalences require correction

Location and classification. Appendix C, p. 17, spatial and temporal flattened-index formulas. Minor mathematical error; confirmed locally, with empirical impact requiring evidence.

The appendix assumes \(0\le i<N\), \(0\le j<F\), \(x=iF+j\), and positive closeness thresholds. It defines spatial support by \(|i_1-i_2|<a_1\), but the flattened expression omits the absolute value. With \(N=2,F=4,a_1=1,x_1=0,x_2=4\), the definition excludes the pair because \(|0-1|=1\), whereas the printed expression accepts it because \(0-1<1\). No causal or ordered-pair restriction is stated. The exact replacement is \[f_s(x_1,x_2)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\left|\left\lfloor x_1/F\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor x_2/F\right\rfloor\right|<a_1.\]

For the temporal support, the modulo condition \(|x_1\bmod F-x_2\bmod F|<a_2\) is correct. The next asserted equivalent condition is existence of an integer \(0\le k<2N-1\) such that \(|(x_1-x_2)-kF|<a_2\). With the same \(N,F\), \(a_2=1\), and \((x_1,x_2)=(0,4)\), the modulo difference is zero but every allowed \(k\) gives a magnitude of at least four. Allowing signed offsets alone is still insufficient: for \(a_2=2\) and \((x_1,x_2)=(3,4)\), the spatial-coordinate difference is three, but \(k=0\) satisfies \(|3-4|=1<2\). This second example also defeats an interpretation that merely renumbers the \(2N-1\) diagonals. The definition uses ordinary, not cyclic, distance between within-frame positions.

A correct exact identity fixes the frame difference rather than existentially choosing it: \[\begin{aligned}
\Delta i&=\lfloor x_1/F\rfloor-\lfloor x_2/F\rfloor,\\
f_t(x_1,x_2)=1&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
|(x_1-x_2)-F\Delta i|<a_2.\end{aligned}\] This follows by substitution of \(x_r=i_rF+j_r\). Alternatively, retain the modulo expression and describe slash bands with their frame-boundary truncations. The reviewer-authored script enumerates all 64 pairs for \(N=2,F=4\) at thresholds 1–4 and verifies the corrected identity without mismatches. The original positive-offset expression disagrees with the defining temporal mask. These examples meet all stated index and threshold assumptions; no learned-attention assumption is used.

Impact and alternatives. The spatial omission is plausibly typographical. The temporal line additionally needs a boundary qualification. Neither error by itself shows that the intended two-pattern method fails. In the later source, :30–46 uses a symmetric absolute-distance mask in reordered coordinates, and lines 61–88 explicitly construct and permute masks. These are block/window implementations, not a literal use of the erroneous existential line. No exact correspondence to the original experimental kernels is established. Repair the appendix and state any block approximation explicitly; evaluating the released GPU kernels and historical runs remains outside this verification.

# Positive checks and implementation qualifications

Section 4.2 attributes layout equivalence to associativity. The more precise fact is permutation equivariance of row-wise softmax. Let \(P\) permute the video tokens, fixing text tokens, and let \(M\) be the correspondingly permuted additive attention mask. Then \[\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(P(QK^\top/\sqrt d+M)P^\top\right)PV
=P\operatorname{softmax}(QK^\top/\sqrt d+M)V.\] Thus undoing the output permutation preserves the masked attention result, provided all three tensors and the mask are transformed consistently. The independent finite example verifies this identity to floating-point precision. It does not assert equivalence between two different window masks. The static index map in :73–100 sends \(iF+j\) to \(jN+i\), preserving the text prefix. Its inverse map at lines 294–303 restores the original coordinates.

Algorithm 1’s expression `.norm().mean(dim=(2,3))` is not a literal per-head PyTorch MSE: an unrestricted norm reduces to a scalar before those dimensions are averaged. Read as schematic pseudocode, its intended comparison is clear. The inspected implementation, :120–145, squares residuals and averages dimensions \((2,3)\), which does implement per-head MSE. Clarifying the pseudocode would prevent a copy-and-run error; the source contradicts any claim that this typo establishes a deployed profiling failure.

The operation-count ratios \(c_s/N\) and \(c_t/F\) in Section 3.3 are retained fractions, despite the label “sparsity.” They are idealized and explicitly omit text/first-frame contributions. End-to-end latency need not equal their reciprocals. Direct arithmetic checks give \(528/237=2.228\), \(528/232=2.276\), \(2253/1171=1.924\), and \(2253/968=2.327\), agreeing with Table 1’s rounded speedups. The FP8 increment is \(1171/968=1.210\), consistent with Figure 7’s 1.21 label; the prose “up to 1.3” is not reproduced here.

# Evidence limits and conclusion

Figure 5, the Table 1 page, Figure 6/7, and Appendix B Figures 9/10 were actually viewed, as was the original Appendix C typography. The supplied frames exhibit similar compositions with visible differences; they cannot establish motion quality over the unavailable full videos. “Lossless” should be understood as a perceptual empirical claim, since the reported finite PSNR and nonzero LPIPS are not bitwise identity. The related-work statement of consistently exceeding 30 dB also should not be generalized to Table 1, whose SVG values include 28.165 and 29.546. Table 3 explicitly uses a random subset, explaining why its larger PSNR is not necessarily inconsistent with Table 1.

The external video folder was not downloaded because its total size could not be bounded during acquisition. OpenReview access returned 403 and was not bypassed. No timing, image metric, raw video, quantized kernel, or statistical uncertainty estimate was independently reproduced. Figures 1–4 and 8 were read through captions/text but not individually image-inspected. No later erratum was established.

The bounded overall assessment is Minor: the appendix’s exact equivalences are false and have an explicit algebraic repair, while the tested layout identity remains valid. Core speed and quality conclusions are neither reproduced nor disproved by these checks. Repair verification is algebraic and finite-example only; author adoption is unknown, scientific adjudication is pending, and human reviews are empty.

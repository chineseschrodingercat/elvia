Abstract: Two Major findings are established within bounded scopes. The claimed emergence of biological pathways beyond both source modalities is not supported by the paper’s own enrichment table at its stated significance threshold. Separately, the unregularized TVN transformation used in the central random-control augmentation is undefined for control sets allowed by the formulation. The independent checks do not establish an author-code crash or invalidate the reported recall improvements. Experimental reproduction, normalization implementation and several statistical details remain Needs Evidence.

# Enrichment evidence does not establish the stated synergy

Problem 1 (The pathway evidence uses the wrong significance threshold and source set: Major).  Section 5.3, pp. 8–9, analyzes relationships unique to distillation, states a filter \(p<0.01\), and uses cell-cycle and post-translational enrichment to argue for biological insights absent from either unimodal source. Table 3, p. 19, does not supply that evidence.

Let \(S\) be the relationships retrieved by Semi-Clipped, \(T\) those retrieved by the transcriptomic baseline, and \(I\) those retrieved by the microscopy baseline. The stated emergence test concerns \[U=S\setminus(T\cup I).\] This is different from morphology-specific transfer, \[V=(S\cap I)\setminus T.\] Indeed \(U\cap V=\varnothing\): every element of \(V\) belongs to \(I\), whereas every element of \(U\) lies outside \(I\).

The original table explicitly gives the following decisive rows:

Pathway | Source set | Reported \(p\)

REACTOME cell cycle checkpoints | \(U\) | 0.0323

REACTOME post-translational protein modification | \(V\) | 0.0002

The cell-cycle-checkpoints row is the only displayed row sourced from \(U\). Its \(p\)-value is 3.23 times the stated cutoff and does not meet \(p<0.01\). All other displayed rows are sourced from \(V\). The post-translational row meets the cutoff, but concerns relationships already retrieved by microscopy and cannot witness emergence beyond the union of the two baselines.

Thus, among the displayed enrichment results, zero pathways simultaneously have the required unique source set and satisfy the stated significance filter. This conclusion uses the actual source-set labels and reported \(p\)-values; it does not require recreating GSEA or making assumptions about unreported gene lists. The parent’s `check.py`/`check.json` records the threshold arithmetic and set distinction.

## Alternatives and counterevidence

Changing the cutoff to 0.05 would admit the cell-cycle-checkpoints result at a nominal level. It would not change the post-translational result’s membership in \(V\), and it would not establish correction for searching many pathways. A raw-versus-adjusted \(p\)-value distinction could in principle explain different thresholds, but the table labels only P-value and supplies no such distinction. Additional unreported qualifying rows would require their actual source sets, statistics and analysis records. These possibilities are Needs Evidence, not verified corrections.

The Venn diagram in Figure 4 still supports substantial overlap and some newly retrieved relationships. For Semi-Clipped the displayed regions give \(|S|=19+21+17+35=92\) and \(|S\cap T|=21+35=56\) out of \(|T|=61\). The 19 relationships in \(U\) are newly retrieved relative to these baselines. Their existence alone does not establish significant pathway enrichment, causal synergy or previously unknown biology; the retrieval task uses known database relationships.

## Dependency and correction

The dependency is direct: Section 5.3 invokes this enrichment analysis to distinguish emergent synergy from ordinary transfer, and Section 6 repeats the conclusion about cell-cycle and post-translational synergies. The supplied evidence fails the very source-set and threshold test used for that distinction. This is a Major defect in a central biological interpretation, even if all recall improvements remain numerically correct.

A supported narrower statement is that Semi-Clipped retrieves additional known relationships and retains morphology-associated pathway information. The stronger unique-pathway claim must be withdrawn or supported by a corrected analysis with explicit background, gene/ranking construction, source sets, threshold and multiplicity handling. No replacement enrichment result is claimed here.

# The declared TVN recipe is not defined on its allowed control sets

Problem 2 (Random-control PEA lacks the rank condition required by its inverse: Major).  Appendix C.3, p. 16 (printed p. 3), defines the full-dimensional TVN map \(T=WD^{-1/2}W^\top\) from the control covariance eigenvalues. The formulation requires only at least two controls and randomizes their number, without a full-rank condition or a stated regularization rule.

Let \(c\) be the number of selected controls and \(m\) the feature dimension. After centering, their data matrix has rank at most \(c-1\). Consequently its \(m\times m\) covariance satisfies \[\operatorname{rank}(\Sigma)\le\min(m,c-1).\] When \(c\le m\), at least one covariance eigenvalue is zero, regardless of numerical precision. Positive variance in each individual coordinate does not ensure a full-rank covariance.

A complete two-dimensional witness is \[X_{\rm control}=
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\-1&-1\end{pmatrix}.\] There are two controls, the mean is zero, and both coordinate standard deviations are one under Appendix C.2’s convention. Centering and scaling are therefore well-defined and leave this matrix unchanged. Its covariance is \[\Sigma=\tfrac12X_{\rm control}^{\top}X_{\rm control}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \operatorname{spec}(\Sigma)=\{2,0\}.\] The prescribed \(D^{-1/2}\) requires \(0^{-1/2}\) and has no finite real value. This example isolates the inverse failure from any zero-coordinate-variance problem. Using an unbiased covariance changes the positive eigenvalue, not the zero.

For the actual 256-dimensional student setting, take the two controls \(\mathbf1_{256}\) and \(-\mathbf1_{256}\). Every coordinate again has positive variance, but the covariance has rank one and nullity 255. More generally, any centered control set with \(c\le256\) has singular covariance. No extra constraint in the inspected formulation excludes these counts or requires affine spanning. Selecting the full TVN sequence is an allowed branch of the augmentation recipe; dropping random steps does not define that branch when it is retained.

The parent independently constructed the two-dimensional covariance and verified its rank and eigenvalues numerically alongside the exact calculation. The defect is mathematical definedness of the supplied normalization, not a claim that the author implementation was observed producing an error.

## What a repair must specify

One option is to restrict every relevant control draw to \(c\ge m+1\), require full column rank after centering and positive feature variance, and reject or handle failing draws. The sample-count condition is necessary, not sufficient: even many controls can be collinear.

Another option is an explicit regularized transformation \[T_\varepsilon=W(D+\varepsilon I)^{-1/2}W^\top,\qquad \varepsilon>0,\] together with a rule for zero coordinate scales, consistent centering/scaling of all embeddings, and a stated row/column convention. This produces a finite covariance inverse square root, but changes the whitening property: \[T_\varepsilon\Sigma T_\varepsilon^\top
=W\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac{\lambda_j}{\lambda_j+\varepsilon}\right)W^\top,\] which is not generally \(I\). For the witness at \(\varepsilon=0.1\), its covariance eigenvalues are exactly \(0\) and \(20/21\); the finite parent diagnostic corroborates this. A pseudoinverse or rank truncation is another possible method, but must specify how null directions are handled. No transformation can turn a rank-one covariance into a full-rank identity by deterministic linear multiplication.

## Impact and uncertainty

This is tied to PEA’s central mechanism: control sampling determines the augmentation delivered to the student, and TVN is also used in the teacher/inference/evaluation description. The stated mapping is undefined for admitted inputs, so its operational assumptions must be strengthened or its formula changed. Under the core-contribution calibration, this supports Major for the described method’s definedness.

There is a substantive counterargument: the appendix could be shorthand for a regularized implementation, making this a missing specification rather than an executed failure. No source code, regularizer, retained-rank parameter or control-count distribution was available to verify that reading. The exact implemented normalization and any effect on the published scores remain Needs Evidence. The assessment does not rely on a difficult repair or claim that all PEA runs fail.

# Checks retained and empirical limitations

## Reported performance and evaluation units

Table 1 reports improved relationship recall for every listed method with PEA across the three datasets. For Semi-Clipped, the parent recalculated relative increases of \(16.95\%\), \(54.81\%\) and \(22.02\%\) from the table’s 15-seed means. Figure 2 and its nearby discussion use five seeds, so their approximately 20% SC-RPE1 figure should not automatically be treated as an arithmetic contradiction with the 15-seed table.

PEA does not improve every preservation entry: SHAKE on SC-RPE1 decreases from 36.95 to 36.15, and VICReg from 36.75 to 36.33. The table itself marks these decreases. Semi-Clipped’s corresponding change is 37.54 to 37.56. These exceptions bound broad preservation rhetoric; they do not negate the reported relationship-recall increases. Table 2’s displayed successive ablation means are monotone for all four methods.

The reported Wilcoxon tests use 15 training seeds. Those seeds measure variation across model-training runs, not 15 independent biological populations or the much larger sample count as independent replicate experiments. The raw paired scores, exact \(p\)-values, test sidedness and multiple-comparison treatment were unavailable. It would be invalid to reconstruct a signed-rank test from marginal means and dispersions alone. No such reconstruction is claimed.

Known-relationship recall first aggregates by perturbed gene, takes top/bottom similarity tails and averages recall over five biological databases. The approximately 10% random baseline is compatible with selecting both 5% tails. This metric measures recovery of curated relationships, not precision of all predicted edges or direct validation of new biological mechanisms. The expression-preservation metric requires a reconstruction probe and structural-distance normalization; its fitting split and original normalization derivation were not independently reproduced.

## Controls, tuning and transfer scope

The teacher is frozen, so the stated update cannot change the teacher representation through the student’s gradient. Metadata-based pairing is explicitly acknowledged even for methods termed label-free; that term refers to the learning objective, not absence of all metadata. Control samples are excluded from the paired distillation data and used for correction. These are meaningful disclosed controls.

The paper describes chemical-perturbation training and genetic-perturbation/out-of-domain evaluation, with a different assay and cell type in additional datasets. Exact sample identifiers, encoder-pretraining overlap, gene-feature harmonization and reconstruction-probe partitions were not available. The hyperparameter study refers to HUVEC-KO while the general protocol mentions a validation split; this is not sufficient evidence to assert test-set leakage.

Appendix A.2 lists different training budgets: 10 epochs for several baselines, 30 for C2KD and 50 for DCCA, versus 150 for Semi-Clipped in the main setup. Validation grid search is stated. Without its search space and convergence records, unequal budgets are a comparison limitation, not proof that baselines were intentionally or materially undertrained.

Inference uses batch controls, and relationship evaluation applies TVN to all output embeddings, while preservation uses raw embeddings. These disclosed choices matter for the practical meaning of unimodal out-of-domain inference. They do not by themselves constitute label leakage. No biological dataset, model, enrichment library or author training program was executed.

# Overall assessment and state

The supported highest assessment is Major. One problem defeats the supplied evidence for emergence beyond the two modalities; the other leaves a central normalization branch undefined under the stated control-count domain. The reported recall gains and evidence of transfer remain separate and are not disproved by these findings.

\section*{Problem 1: Original-resolution subtraction adds a resizing residual absent from Equation (4)}

Source: Section 3.2, Eq. (4), PDF p. 6; released optimize\_image.py extraction, lines 50–55.

Assessment: supported; impact: minor

\subsection*{Source claim (paraphrase)}

Section 3.2, Eq. (4), PDF p. 6, forms a perturbation at the resized resolution and then upsamples that perturbation for transfer. Released optimize\_image.py, lines 50–55, instead subtracts an upsampled optimized image from the original-resolution reference.

\subsection*{Analysis}

Let \(D\) downsample, \(U\) upsample, \(x\) be the original image and \(z\) the optimized low-resolution image. The specified perturbation is \(U(Dx-z)\), while the helper uses \(x-Uz\); their difference is \(x-UDx\). Setting \(z=Dx\) eliminates optimization changes, but the helper still assigns ordinary lost resize detail as a nonzero perturbation. Quantization is not needed for this discrepancy.

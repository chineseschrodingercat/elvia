\section*{Problem 1: The historical recipe placed entropy\_top\_ratio outside the actor configuration}

Source: Eq. (2); current actor recipe, actor configuration declaration and dp\_actor.py lines 440–461.

Assessment: not\_issue; impact: no\_issue\_found

\subsection*{Source claim (paraphrase)}

Eq. (2) selects response tokens using ratio \(\rho=0.2\). The historical recipe supplies that setting through actor\_rollout\_ref.model.entropy\_top\_ratio, but verl/workers/fsdp\_workers.py, lines 785–789, constructs the actor from the separate actor configuration branch. In verl/workers/actor/dp\_actor.py, line 440, the actor looks up entropy\_top\_ratio in its own configuration and bypasses the helper when the setting is absent.

\subsection*{Analysis}

The historical recipe places entropy\_top\_ratio equal to \(0.2\) in the model branch, but the worker constructs its actor from the actor branch and the actor looks up the key there. That lookup yields None and bypasses high-entropy selection in favor of the full-response mask. The authors' subsequent fix moves both the setting and its structured declaration into the actor configuration.

At the current head, the recipe explicitly sets actor\_rollout\_ref.actor.entropy\_top\_ratio to \(0.2\), the actor dataclass/YAML declares the field, and the worker passes the actor branch. The historical missing-key lookup no longer applies.

\subsection*{Finding and scope}

The known configuration-delivery concern is already corrected in the current release. The author statement that published runs were unaffected is recorded as attribution, without independent recovery of their experiment provenance.

\section*{Problem 2: An integer response mask was interpreted as index positions}

Source: Current verl/trainer/ppo/core\_algos.py, get\_global\_entropy\_top\_mask, lines 53–87.

Assessment: not\_issue; impact: no\_issue\_found

\subsection*{Source claim (paraphrase)}

Historical verl/trainer/ppo/core\_algos.py, get\_global\_entropy\_top\_mask, lines 53–87, flattens the response mask and uses it directly to index the flattened entropy tensor. The declared mask marks eligible response tokens, but an integer-valued mask has integer-index rather than Boolean-mask semantics. The author correction explicitly converts it to Boolean first.

\subsection*{Analysis}

For entropy values \((0.9,0.1)\) and an integer mask \((1,0)\), integer indexing gathers positions one and zero, yielding \((0.1,0.9)\), rather than the eligible singleton \((0.9)\). Subsequent selection can therefore map to the wrong token. The existing Boolean conversion changes the operation to eligibility filtering, as required.

Current line 68 explicitly converts the flattened mask to Boolean. For the same witness it filters the eligible singleton \((0.9)\) and maps its selected index back to the first position, yielding \((1,0)\). No integer-index ambiguity remains.

\subsection*{Finding and scope}

The current Boolean conversion removes the historical indexing witness. This does not certify arbitrary rounding boundaries, distributed execution or complete training.

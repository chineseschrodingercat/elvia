Abstract: This report examines the acquired author-hosted manuscript of Mao, Wan and Zhang, with emphasis on its local smooth-transfer constructions, Fourier factors and representation-theoretic bookkeeping. One repeated normalization error is established in the explicit test functions in the proofs of Theorems 4.4(1) and 5.9(1). Exact admissible examples and a verified repair are given. The required range statements survive by linearity and, in the bilinear case, by a direct uniform-support construction; no counterexample to the local main theorem is established. A table convention and an unresolved Model 6 normalization are distinguished from proved errors. The initial assessment is Minor within the inspected scope. Scientific adjudication and independent acceptance remain pending.

# Problem 1: incorrect volume multipliers in two local constructions

## Exact claim and first occurrence

Equation reference: eq:Jordan. The first occurrence is the first proof paragraph of Theorem 4.4(1), p. 18; the defining integral follows Proposition 4.2 on p. 17: \[
 I(a,\phi)=\int_{J(F)}\phi(a,A)
 \psi\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{tr}A-N_J(A)}{a}\right)\,dA,\qquad a\in F^\times.\] The proof chooses \(B=a_0+\varpi^m\mathcal O_F\), with \(|a|=|a_0|\) on \(B\), and a small compact open \(K\subset J(F)\) so that the phase is trivial. It instructs multiplication of the indicator of \(B\times K\) by the volume of the set, and claims \(I(a,\phi)=1_B(a)\).

Equation reference: eq:scalar. For \(\phi=c\,1_B1_K\) under the stipulated phase condition, direct integration gives \[
 I(a,\phi)=c\,\operatorname{vol}(K)\,1_B(a).\] The variable \(a\) is a parameter, not an integration variable. The required coefficient is therefore \(\operatorname{vol}(K)^{-1}\), without a factor involving \(\operatorname{vol}(B)\).

For a fully admissible instance, take Model 1, \(F=\mathbb Q_5\), \(J=\mathrm{Mat}_3(F)\), \(\psi\) of conductor \(\mathbb Z_5\), and product self-dual measure with \(\operatorname{vol}(\mathbb Z_5)=1\). For any integer \(m\ge1\), put \[a_0=1,\qquad B=1+5^m\mathbb Z_5,\qquad
 K=5^m\mathrm{Mat}_3(\mathbb Z_5).\] All \(a\in B\) are units, \(\operatorname{tr}A\in5^m\mathbb Z_5\), and \(\det A\in5^{3m}\mathbb Z_5\). Thus the character in [eq:Jordan] (#eq:Jordan) is identically one on the proposed support, and all compactness and local-constancy requirements hold. Since \(\operatorname{vol}(B)=5^{-m}\) and \(\operatorname{vol}(K)=5^{-9m}\), the literal coefficient \(c=\operatorname{vol}(B\times K)\) gives \[I(a,\phi)=5^{-19m}1_B(a)\ne1_B(a).\] Reading the coefficient as \(\operatorname{vol}(K)\) instead yields \(5^{-18m}\), which also fails. Taking \(m\) arbitrarily large excludes an implicit sufficiently-small-support exception. The corrected function \[\phi(a,A)=\operatorname{vol}(K)^{-1}1_B(a)1_K(A)\] has the claimed integral and has both singular values \(\phi(0,I)\) and \(\phi(0,-I)\) equal to zero.

## Second occurrence and exact countercalculation

Equation reference: eq:FJ. The same instruction appears in the first proof paragraph of Theorem 5.9(1), p. 26. Its integral is the unnumbered formula in Proposition 5.7, p. 25, rather than the Weil-action equation (5.7): \[
 I(a,\phi,\phi')=\int_J\int_{Y'}
 \psi\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{tr}A-N_J(A)-\langle Y,A\rangle}{a}
 -\frac{\langle A,A\rangle}{2a^2}\right)
 \phi(a,A)\phi'(Y)\,dY\,dA .\] For Model 5, \(\dim J=15\), \(\dim Y'=4\), and \(\langle A,A\rangle=A_2A_5+A_3A_4\) in the manuscript’s coordinates.

Equation reference: eq:wrong39. Take \(F=\mathbb Q_3\), an unramified \(\psi\), normalized product measure, and \[B=1+3^M\mathbb Z_3,\quad K=(3^M\mathbb Z_3)^{15},\quad
 K'=(3^M\mathbb Z_3)^4,\qquad M\ge1.\] The four phase terms have respective valuations at least \(M,3M,2M,2M\). Both \(a\) and \(2\) are units. Hence the phase in [eq:FJ] (#eq:FJ) is integral throughout the support and the character is one. Write \(b=3^{-M}\), \(k=3^{-15M}\), \(k'=3^{-4M}\). The literal volume multipliers \(bk\) for \(\phi\) and \(k'\) for \(\phi'\) give \[
 I(a,\phi,\phi')=(bk)k'\,kk'\,1_B(a)
 =3^{-39M}1_B(a)\ne1_B(a).\] At \(M=1\), the scalar is exactly \(1/4052555153018976267\). Independent rational arithmetic reproduced this value. If the first multiplier means \(k\) alone, the scalar is instead \(3^{-38M}\), still incorrect.

## Verified repair, counterevidence and impact

Equation reference: eq:repair. For any \(f\in C_c^\infty(F^\times)\), its support is compact and bounded away from zero. Consequently the coefficients containing \(a^{-1}\) and \(a^{-2}\) in [eq:FJ] (#eq:FJ) are uniformly bounded on that support. Choose common sufficiently small \(K,K'\) about zero so that the character is identically one there for every \(a\in\operatorname{supp}(f)\). Then \[
 \phi(a,A)=f(a)\frac{1_K(A)}{\operatorname{vol}(K)},\qquad
 \phi'(Y)=\frac{1_{K'}(Y)}{\operatorname{vol}(K')}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad I(a,\phi,\phi')=f(a).\] This proves the required realization by a single pair, without assuming that an arbitrary sum of pure tensors is itself pure. Dividing by \(\operatorname{vol}(B\times K)\) would not be the correct repair: it would leave an unwanted factor \(\operatorname{vol}(B)^{-1}\).

The established defect is the false exact identity for the stated test functions. Its assessment is Minor. This classification rests on the actual proof dependency: the local argument needs the image to contain \(C_c^\infty(F^\times)\); the printed witnesses already yield nonzero multiples of the required indicators, and linearity gives this image in Section 4. Formula [eq:repair] (#eq:repair) directly supplies it in Section 5. The germ computation and basic-function fundamental lemma are separate calculations. No failure of the required range, transfer exponent, or singular distribution follows from the wrong multiplier. The classification is not based merely on the repair being short, and it is not a claim that a Major issue would require an irreparable or globally false theorem.

# Positive local checks and their limits

## Character orthogonality and matching germs

For the unramified character and self-dual measure in (4.2), p. 18, \[\int_{\varpi^m\mathcal O_F}\int_{\varpi^m\mathcal O_F}
 \psi(bxy)\,dx\,dy=|b|^{-1}\qquad (|b|>q^{2m}).\] Indeed the inner integral is \(q^{-m}\) exactly when \(y\in b^{-1}\varpi^{-m}\mathcal O_F\). The stated inequality makes this ball a subset of \(\varpi^m\mathcal O_F\); its volume is \(|b|^{-1}q^m\). This verifies the identity analytically.

Definition 3.1 and Proposition 3.3 (p. 15) normalize the Kuznetsov function as \(|a|J(a)\), while Theorem 4.4 uses \(|a|^{(1-n)/2}I(a)\). Equality of these expressions gives exactly \(I(a)=|a|^{(n+1)/2}J(a)\). The two singular coefficients must both be matched. The chosen condition \(|2|>|\varpi^m|\) separates the neighbourhoods of \(I\) and \(-I\), including dyadic characteristic-zero fields after increasing \(m\). Independent local bump functions prescribe the two values. After their germs agree, the difference vanishes near zero and is boundedly supported, so the away-from-zero range result supplies the remaining correction.

For Model 1, the stationary equation for \(\operatorname{tr}A-\det A\) is \(\operatorname{adj}(A)=I\). It forces \(A=\det(A)I\) and \(\det(A)^2=1\), hence \(A=\pm I\). Off these points, a nonzero coordinate derivative and separate affine dependence of the determinant permit character-orthogonality cancellation on small balls. This validates the inspected nonstationary step in that model. The corresponding Jordan calculations in every other model and the full general basic-function computation remain dependencies on the manuscript’s cited work [21].

 \zeta_p^{\operatorname{tr}A-\det A}
 =p^{-5}\sum_{x\in\mathbb F_p^\times}\zeta_p^{x+x^{-1}}.\] The exact phase-count vectors, followed by their difference after subtracting \(p^4\) times the Kloosterman counts, were

The differences are multiples of the relevant cyclotomic relations. These checks support the normalization for the specified fields and single valuation only; they do not prove the general fundamental lemma. They are concrete counterevidence against attributing its failure to Problem 1.

## Fourier–Jacobi factors in Model 5

On p. 27, Fourier integration in \(Y'\simeq F^4\) forces \(A_i\in a\mathcal O_F\) for \(i=2,\ldots,5\). When the residual characteristic is not two, the remaining quadratic phase is integral, justifying the stated restriction. The four paired integrations in (5.15), p. 28, contribute \(|a|^4\). On p. 29 the four Fourier restrictions contribute \(q^{-4m}\), and \(A_i=aB_i\) contributes \(|a|^4\), agreeing with the progression \(|a|^3\) to \(|a|^7\). The earlier \(|a|^3\) reduction imported from [21] was not independently reconstructed.

For a sufficiently large ball \(D=\varpi^{-m}\mathcal O_F\), \[\int_D\int_D\psi(-xy/2)\,dx\,dy
 =\operatorname{vol}(D)\operatorname{vol}(D\cap 2\varpi^m\mathcal O_F)=|2|.\] The four-variable quadratic factor on p. 30 consists of two such pairs, hence equals \(|4|\). Completing the bilinear products translates by fixed centres contained in \(D\) for sufficiently large \(m\). Exact finite character-orthogonality checks at \(p=2,3,5\), \(m=1,2\), gave the four-variable factors \(1/4,1,1\), respectively. The Model 5 factor \(|4|\) is therefore supported, including at the checked dyadic place. This is consistent with the germ coefficients \(c_\pm=|4|I^\pm\) in Theorem 5.9(3) and the transfer factor in Conjecture 5.8.

# Representation bookkeeping and conjectural scope

## Model 1 in Table 3

Equation reference: eq:table. This is a scope clarification, not an additional substantive Problem. On p. 2 the decomposition is defined for the full adjoint representation. The Model 1 nilpotent on p. 12 has root vectors \(E_{31},E_{42},E_{53},E_{64}\), with two Jordan chains of length three. Thus, over the complex representation category relevant here, \[V=U\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^2W,\qquad \dim U=\dim W=2.\] The multiplicity-space action induces \(H_0=\mathrm{PGL}_2\). Put \(A=\operatorname{Ad}_{\mathrm{PGL}_2}\), of dimension three. The full decomposition is \[
 \mathfrak{sl}_6
 =(A\otimes\mathbf1)
 \oplus((A\oplus\mathbf1)\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^2W)
 \oplus((A\oplus\mathbf1)\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^4W).\] Its dimension is \(3+4\cdot3+4\cdot5=35\). Exact matrix ranks \(4,2,0\) for the first three nilpotent powers and an independent comparison of every torus weight verified this calculation.

On its most favourable reading, the first row of Table 3 (p. 13) lists the nontrivial \(H_0\)-isotypic part, of dimension \(27\). The omitted eight dimensions are \(\mathbf1\otimes(\operatorname{Sym}^2W\oplus\operatorname{Sym}^4W)\). Restoring them introduces precisely \(\zeta_k(2)\zeta_k(3)\) in Conjecture 2.3 (p. 14). Remark 1.2 expressly permits suppression of Dedekind zeta factors, so this does not contradict the conjecture at its stated normalization precision. Both readings retain only the indices \(0,2,4\), preserving the relevant parity assumption. An explicit nontrivial-isotypic convention, or the full coefficients in [eq:table] (#eq:table), would remove the ambiguity. No damage to the local main theorem was established.

## Section 6 branching and the formal comparison

In Section 6.2 (p. 31), the standard representation of \(\mathrm{SO}_{4n+1}(\mathbb C)\) restricts as \((V\otimes W)\oplus\mathbf1\), with \(V\) the \(2n\)-dimensional symplectic standard module and \(W\) the two-dimensional \(\mathrm{SL}_2\) module. The exterior-square identity gives \[\operatorname{Ad}_{\mathrm{SO}_{4n+1}}
 =\operatorname{Ad}_{\mathrm{Sp}_{2n}}\otimes\mathbf1
 \oplus V\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^1W
 \oplus\Lambda^2V\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^2W.\] The dimensions sum to \(n(2n+1)+4n+3n(2n-1)=8n^2+2n\), as required. The odd coefficient is \(V\), and the denominator shifts in Conjecture 6.1 are consequently \(1,3/2,2\), matching the displayed formula. Formally multiplying the normalized squared-period expressions in Conjectures 6.1 and 6.2 gives the stated \(L(1/2)^2/L(1,\operatorname{Ad})^2\) pattern, at the declared precision of suppressed factors. This algebra does not prove either conjecture or an unnormalized equality of periods. Proposition 6.4 (pp. 33–34) retains the external root-exchange and vanishing dependencies [9] and [10]; those proofs were not independently re-established.

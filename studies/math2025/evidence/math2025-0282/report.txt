Neither witness disproves the corresponding existence lemma or the main classification and determinacy conclusions. Scientific adjudication remains pending.

# Claim, assumptions and dependencies

The paper studies subsets of Baire space and their characteristic functions. Class descriptions are well-founded labelled trees, with an oracle computing the ordinal data; names use computable forests and ordinal-bounded approximations. The ordinal codes and their initial-segment convention in Section 2.2 matter: claims of uniformity are stronger than statements merely about ordinal order types. The report does not replace effective containment by ordinary set inclusion.

The main chain is: true stages and forest representations; effective operations on names; universal functions and non-self-duality (Proposition 3.14); classification of acceptable descriptions (Theorem 4.4); the separation theorem (Theorem 5.1); and classification of all Borel Wadge classes and determinacy (Theorems 6.8–6.10). Section 6 explicitly adds \(\Pi^1_1\) induction at the minimal-description step. Its distinction between no infinite descending sequence and existence of minimal elements on real-coded objects is appropriate and was retained in the review.

For \(\alpha=0\), the true-stage order is ordinary prefix order. Definition 2.4 permits any convex set of finite strings as a forest, including finite components with no infinite path. In particular, disjointness of the represented sets of infinite paths does not imply that the finite representing forests are disjoint. This is the relevant boundary case for Problem 1.

# Substantive findings

Problem 1 (Simultaneous entry is not excluded by Lemma 2.11).  Major: a failed effective construction used in the core proof chain.

## Source and witness

Lemma 2.11, p. 8, starts with uniformly computable sets \(U_i\) that are \(\alpha\)-open in \(S\), representing pairwise disjoint sets \(Z_i\). After arranging \(|\sigma|_\alpha>i\) on \(U_i\), it recursively places \(\sigma\) in \(W_i\) if \(\sigma\in U_i\) and no proper \(\alpha\)-predecessor of \(\sigma\) belongs to a competing \(W_j\), for \(j<|\sigma|_\alpha\). There is no rule for two sets claiming the same string at the same height.

Here is a witness with a nonempty represented domain. Set \(\alpha=0\) and write \(1^n\) for the length-\(n\) string of ones. Let \[S=\{\langle0,0\rangle\}\cup\{1^n:n\geq1\},\qquad
 U_0=S,\quad U_1=\{\langle0,0\rangle\},\quad U_i=\varnothing\ (i\geq2).\] The set \(S\) is a computable prefix-convex forest. Both \(U_0\) and \(U_1\) are computable and open in \(S\) in the precise sense of Definition 2.2. The height conditions hold: every element of \(U_0\) has positive length, and the sole element of \(U_1\) has length two. Their represented sets are \[[S]_0=Z_0=\{1^\omega\},\qquad Z_1=Z_2=\cdots=\varnothing.\] These are uniformly effectively open relative to \([S]_0\), pairwise disjoint, and even form a partition of that nonempty space. Thus no extra nonemptiness convention on the domain rescues the printed recursion.

The string \(\sigma=\langle0,0\rangle\) has no proper predecessor in \(S\). Every \(W_j\) is a subset of \(S\), so the exclusion test is false for both \(i=0\) and \(i=1\). Consequently \[\sigma\in W_0\cap W_1.\] Definition 2.10 requires every cross-pair to be incomparable, and a string is comparable to itself. The constructed forests are therefore not orthogonal. The independently written finite check retains the first eight strings on the infinite component and verifies both memberships at \(\langle0,0\rangle\); the analytic argument above establishes the infinite-domain hypotheses without relying on truncation.

## Impact and counterevidence

This is a counterexample to the displayed construction, not to the existence assertion of Lemma 2.11. For this particular input, taking \(W_0=S\) and \(W_i=\varnothing\) for \(i>0\) already proves the existence assertion. Nevertheless, the printed uniform construction supplies an essential guarantee: orthogonality makes a later rule assigning the unique index at a node well-defined. It is used in gluing (Proposition 3.17), the limit case of Proposition 4.9 (p. 28), construction of the separator name (p. 34), and the proof of Proposition 2.14 (p. 49). The gap is thus on the main effective proof route, even though a local repair is available.

## A repair with its invariant

For each \(\sigma\in S\), inspect its finitely many \(\alpha\)-predecessors in \(S\), including itself. Find the earliest such \(\rho\) belonging to at least one \(U_i\), and at that \(\rho\) assign the least eligible index \(i\). Keep that owner at all extensions in \(S\). If there is no hit, give no owner. The height bound makes each search over indices finite and uniformly computable.

The resulting \(W_i\) are open in \(S\): comparable strings that have an owner have the same first hit and owner. They are pairwise orthogonal and lie in \(U_i\), since each \(U_i\) is upward closed in \(S\). Their union is computable. On a path \(x\in Z_i\), some true prefix lies in \(U_i\); its owner cannot be \(j\ne i\), since that would give \(x\in Z_j\) as well. Hence \([W_i]_\alpha=Z_i\). These invariants should replace the missing simultaneous-entry argument. They use the computability of the representing forest implicit in the effective applications; the exhibited witness already satisfies that stronger assumption.

Problem 2 (The totalization reads the wrong argument).  Major: failure of totality in the universal-name construction.

## Exact statement and failed step

Lemma 3.11, p. 13, permits partial \(z\)-computable input functions \(f\) and \(\beta\). It promises total output functions \(g\) and \(\gamma\) regardless of whether the inputs satisfy the additional validity assumptions in clause (iv). At a nonempty string \(\sigma\), the proof searches for a longest \(\rho\leq_\xi^z\sigma\) such that both \(f(\rho)\) and \(\beta(\rho)\) converge within \(k=|\sigma|_\xi^z\) steps and \(\beta(\rho)<\gamma(\tau)\), where \(\tau\) is the immediate predecessor. When such a \(\rho\) is found, the printed assignment is \[g(\sigma)=f(\sigma),\qquad \gamma(\sigma)=\beta(\sigma).\] The search has certified convergence at \(\rho\), not at \(\sigma\).

Even after generously correcting the preceding initialization from \(f(\varnothing)=n^*\) to \(g(\varnothing)=n^*\), this assignment is invalid. Choose \(z\) computable, \(\xi=0\), \(\eta=1\), and \(n^*=0\). Let both input programs return \(0\) on the empty string and diverge on every nonempty string. They are legitimate partial computable functions of the required types. Let \(T\) be a finite positive bound by which both empty-input computations halt. Along any prefix chain, before the first stage admitting this convergence the output is still the default with \(\gamma=1\). At that first eligible stage the only possible selected ancestor is \(\rho=\varnothing\), with \(\beta(\rho)=0<1\). The displayed rule then asks for \(f(\sigma)\) and \(\beta(\sigma)\) at a nonempty \(\sigma\), so fails to return. This contradicts the promised unconditional totality.

The saved independent check uses an explicit partial-value table and an illustrative finite convergence bound \(T=3\). It evaluates the printed selection and records the undefined requested lookup, rather than pretending to decide general halting. The mathematical nontermination assertion follows from the explicit input program specification; the number three is a chosen demonstration bound, not a measured step count for the paper’s universal machine.

## Dependency and repair

The text immediately uses Lemma 3.11 to enumerate total names; Lemmas 3.12–3.13 then construct a universal function, and Proposition 3.14 obtains non-self-duality by diagonalization. Later separation and minimal-class arguments repeatedly use that universal function. Thus totality is a necessary core guarantee, not a harmless output-format issue. There is no demonstrated counterexample to those existence theorems or to Borel Wadge determinacy.

Initialize \(g(\varnothing)=n^*\) and retain the supplied \(f\) unchanged. In the successful branch use \[g(\sigma)=f(\rho),\qquad
                  \gamma(\sigma)=\beta(\rho).\] The values are now certified by the bounded search. The finite predecessor search is computable; each change strictly lowers the ordinal witness, and the default persists at the top value. Along a true path the output therefore stabilizes. For valid total input, if the eventual output disagreed with the input limit, either it would still be the default at height \(\eta\) or it would have last sampled a true ancestor with a different value. The input default condition in clause (iv) in the first case and the input convergence-witness condition in the second give a later true prefix with \(\beta\) strictly below the current \(\gamma\). Eventually its computation is seen by the bounded search, forcing another update, a contradiction. This supplies the missing justification for clause (iv). It should be written explicitly and then propagated through the universal-name construction.

# Other checks, limits and recommended response

The zero-type convention, default branch, successor/limit distinction in Proposition 4.9, and the different notions of effective and ordinary containment were checked while reading. The game proof tracks two different oracles for the original name and the separator; footnote 4 on p. 34 explicitly addresses this, so it was not treated as an oracle inconsistency. The later induction on descriptions and the passage through hyperarithmetic codes were traced without claiming a separate formalization in second-order arithmetic.

Several other symbol/index slips were encountered, but were not promoted to additional findings without a distinct verified mathematical effect. Nor is the finite computation in Problem 1 a substitute for proving the effective transfinite constructions. The two defects above suffice to request correction of the relevant proof steps and an author check of their dependents in this version. Both are classified by their role in the proof, not downgraded solely because the replacements are short. The appropriate overall initial technical assessment is major proof corrections required for the inspected v1; human scientific adjudication remains pending.

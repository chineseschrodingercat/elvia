Assigned journal DOI: `10.1007/s00222-025-01376-3`. All page references are one-based PDF pages, equal to printed pages. This review does not certify an unexamined journal revision.

# Scope and outcome

One Major proof-level gap was established in the full-range global-regularity deduction of Theorem 1.6. The interpolation on p. 29 cannot supply the required continuation exponent for some kernels allowed by the theorem. Below are a kernel/parameter example satisfying the stated hypotheses and a smooth positive family disproving the relevant norm implication. Neither is a blow-up example for the Boltzmann equation.

The conditional Fisher-information argument was checked through the product lifting, radial square completion and angular constants, with no issue found in that chain. All 35 pages were read as extracted text and decisive evidence pages were inspected visually. This is a claim-focused initial review, not an independent reproof of every lemma or imported theorem.

# Definitions and core proof checks

Equation reference: eq:key, eq:rad. For \(B(|v-w|,\cos\theta)=\alpha(|v-w|/2)b(\cos\theta)\), define \(\mathcal Bf(\sigma)=\int(f(\sigma')-f(\sigma))b(\sigma'\cdot\sigma)\,d\sigma'\). Theorem 1.2 (p. 3) uses the best constant \(\Lambda_b\) in \[
 \int f\,\Gamma^2_{\mathcal B,\Delta}(\log f,\log f)\,d\sigma
 \geq\Lambda_b\iint\frac{(f'-f)^2}{f'+f}b(\sigma'\cdot\sigma)\,d\sigma'\,d\sigma\] for positive even functions on \(S^{d-1}\), and assumes \[
 r|\alpha'(r)|/\alpha(r)\leq2\sqrt{\Lambda_b}.\] For \(\alpha=r^\gamma\), this is \(|\gamma|\leq2\sqrt{\Lambda_b}\). Section 2.2 (p. 8) restricts the differentiations to smooth, positive, sufficiently decaying solutions; there is no unqualified weak-solution assertion here.

Equation reference: eq:square. For \(F=f\otimes f\), exchange symmetry becomes angular evenness under \(v=z+r\sigma,\ w=z-r\sigma\). The product/marginal identity is \(\langle i'(f),q(f)\rangle=\tfrac12\langle I'(F),Q(F)\rangle\). For \(S=F+F'>0\), \(D=F'-F\), \(X=\partial_r\log F-\partial_r\log F'\), the radial calculation on pp. 10–11 is the valid identity \[
 -\frac{\alpha S}{2}X^2+\alpha'XD
 =-\frac{\alpha S}{2}\left(X-\frac{\alpha'D}{\alpha S}\right)^2
 +\frac{(\alpha')^2D^2}{2\alpha S}
 \leq\frac{(\alpha')^2D^2}{2\alpha S}.\] On p. 16, angular dissipation has coefficient \(4\alpha r^{d-3}\) multiplying the left side of [eq:key] (#eq:key), and the radial remainder has coefficient \((\alpha')^2r^{d-1}/\alpha\). Thus [eq:rad] (#eq:rad), including its factor 2, is sufficient. Common positive coordinate normalization factors do not change this sign comparison.

Equation reference: eq:frac. The constants on p. 23 give \(2c_K/C_P=d-2\). For subordinate kernels, pp. 25–28 give \[c_K=\tfrac12\int_0^\infty\omega(t)(1-e^{-2\Lambda_{\rm local}t})\,dt,\qquad
 C_P=\tfrac1d\int_0^\infty\omega(t)(1-e^{-2dt})\,dt.\] These yield the stated final ratio \(2c_K/C_P\). For the fractional kernel, Corollary 9.10 gives \[
 \Lambda_{b_s}\geq\Lambda_{\rm local}^{\,s}d^{1-s},
 \qquad \Lambda_{\rm local}=d+3-\frac1{d-1}.\] The imported projective-space estimate in Theorem 9.4 was not independently reproved. Evenness is essential, and the general \(d-2\) estimate gives no positive lower bound in dimension two.

# Problem 1: an unavailable continuation exponent

Major — proof gap. Evidence: Theorem 1.6, p. 5; Theorem 2.1, p. 8; Section 10, pp. 28–29; kernel conditions and constants, pp. 4,25–26,28,30.

Equation reference: eq:int, eq:crit, eq:range. In dimension three, \(i(f)=4\|\nabla\sqrt f\|_2^2\) controls \(f\) in \(L^3\). Interpolation with a moment of order \(k_2\) yields \[
 \int\langle v\rangle^{k_2(3-p)/2}f^p\,dv
 \leq
 \left(\int\langle v\rangle^{k_2}f\,dv\right)^{(3-p)/2}
 \left(\int f^3\,dv\right)^{(p-1)/2},\qquad 1\leq p\leq3.\] The two powers must be nonnegative. Higher moments increase a velocity weight, not the maximum admissible \(p\). The continuation criterion in Theorem 2.1 requires \[
 p_1>\frac3{3+\gamma+2s},\qquad k_1=1-\frac{3(\gamma+2s)}{2s}.\] The choice \(p=3/(3+\gamma+2s)+\) on p. 29 is possible in [eq:int] (#eq:int) only if \[
 \gamma+2s>-2.\] Equality fails too because the continuation inequality is strict. Theorem 1.6 does not assume [eq:range] (#eq:range).

## The Fisher hypothesis does not exclude the problematic range

Set \(d=3,\ s=1/8,\ \gamma=-5/2\). Let \(b_s\) be the fractional Laplace–Beltrami kernel and let \(b(c)=(b_s(c)+b_s(-c))/2\). The even-function constant is unchanged by symmetrization (p. 4). The fractional-kernel endpoint asymptotic discussed on p. 30 gives \(b(\cos\theta)\asymp|\sin\theta|^{-2-2s}\), including both endpoints after symmetrization. Angular integrability holds since \(s<1\). By [eq:frac] (#eq:frac), \[\Lambda_b\geq(11/2)^{1/8}3^{7/8}>3,\qquad
 |\gamma|=5/2<2\sqrt3<2\sqrt{\Lambda_b}.\] All the displayed kernel/range and Fisher assumptions of Theorem 1.6 hold, but \[\gamma+2s=-9/4,\qquad p_1>4,\qquad k_1=28 .\]

## A direct counterexample to the norm implication

Equation reference: eq:fam. Choose \(\psi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^3)\), \(0\leq\psi\leq1\), equal to 1 on \(B_1\) and zero outside \(B_2\). For \(0<\varepsilon<1\), let \[
 f_\varepsilon(v)=\psi(v)^2(|v|^2+\varepsilon^2)^{-2/5}+e^{-|v|^2}.\] Each is smooth, strictly positive and individually belongs to every polynomially weighted \(L^\infty\) space. Every fixed mass moment is uniformly bounded: the compactly supported part is bounded by \(|v|^{-4/5}\) off the origin.

Equation reference: eq:div. Fisher information is uniformly bounded as well. Pointwise Cauchy–Schwarz gives \(I(a+b)\leq I(a)+I(b)\), with the continuous interpretation at zeros. The square root of the first term is \(\psi(v)(|v|^2+\varepsilon^2)^{-1/5}\). Apart from a bounded cutoff contribution, its Dirichlet integral is bounded by \[C\int_0^2r^4(r^2+\varepsilon^2)^{-12/5}\,dr
 \leq C\int_0^2r^{-4/5}\,dr<\infty .\] The Gaussian contribution is fixed. The \(L^3\) norm is also uniformly bounded since its limiting radial exponent is \(2-12/5=-2/5>-1\). On \(|v|\leq\varepsilon\), however, \[
 \int f_\varepsilon^p\,dv\geq
 \frac{4\pi}{3}\,2^{-2p/5}\varepsilon^{\,3-4p/5}\longrightarrow\infty
 \quad(p>15/4).\] In particular this diverges for every \(p>4\), and the required weight \(\langle v\rangle^{28}\geq1\) cannot help. Bounded positive normalization factors can enforce a common mass.

This family refutes the stated norm inference, not global existence: the functions are not asserted to solve Boltzmann. Additional dynamical estimates might repair the full theorem, but are not supplied by the interpolation on p. 29.

## Impact and limited repair

The full-range global-regularity deduction is affected. The conditional Fisher theorem is not disproved. For the physical three-dimensional inverse-power-law relation \(\gamma=(q-5)/(q-1),\ s=1/(q-1),\ q>2\), one has \(\gamma+2s\in(-1,1)\); this particular obstruction does not arise.

A restricted repair adds [eq:range] (#eq:range), chooses \[\max\{1,3/(3+\gamma+2s)\}<p<3,\qquad
 k_2>\max\{2,2k_1/(3-p)\},\] and assumes sufficient initial polynomial decay for that moment and the imported local theory. Then [eq:int] (#eq:int) supplies the weighted continuation norm. This verifies a restricted proof, not the original full-range claim. Author adoption has not been sought.

# Governance

Minhao Liu ([@chineseschrodingercat](https://github.com/chineseschrodingercat)) is
ELVIA's founder and sole maintainer. He manages software, releases, contribution
intake and publication of project records.

Scientific contributions are reviewed through the website. Named credit is
optional. Reviewers identify their expertise, conflicts and relationship to the
source paper. Author responses and rebuttals remain attributable to their
contributors. The project does not currently have an independent appeal panel
or a confidential scientific intake service.

Editorial acceptance does not change a finding's scientific status. The
[protocol](docs/protocol.md) requires substantive independent human review for
confirmed decisions, with two agreeing domain reviewers for a material main-result
or major issue. Software verifies the recorded declarations; the maintainer
checks the reviewers' qualifications and the underlying evidence.

Scientific records retain supporting evidence, counterevidence, scope and earlier
versions. Issue decisions, repair verification and author adoption are recorded
separately. Silence does not establish agreement or adoption. A disputed finding
can be corrected or left unresolved while further review is sought.

Process changes are discussed in Issues and reviewed in pull requests. Software
integration requires all five CI checks at the final integration commit. The
maintainer records actual checks and deployment results; branch protection is
described as enforced only after its current repository setting is verified.

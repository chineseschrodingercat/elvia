## Short introduction

ELVIA is building open infrastructure for examining the scientific claims that
future research depends on. Its public Atlas connects specific paper passages to
AI-assisted analyses, counterevidence and the limits of each conclusion.
Researchers can inspect the reasoning and contribute focused checks. Independent
scientific review of the initial collection remains pending.

## Mission statement

Scientific progress depends on results that others can understand and use. A
proof step, measurement or assumption can influence what later researchers
attempt. ELVIA's ambition is to make these foundations open to scrutiny before
they are carried into further work.

The work begins with precise questions about published claims. An analysis
identifies the source and conditions, develops an inspectable argument, and
preserves counterevidence and unresolved issues. AI can assist this work;
independent scientific review determines the status of a finding. Versioned
records preserve earlier interpretations and later revisions.

The long-term aim is shared infrastructure that helps researchers make
better-informed decisions about what to cite, extend and put into practice.

## Selected-case posts

### A geometric comparison

> Can two different lines become indistinguishable in a comparison used by a
> proof?
>
> ELVIA's preliminary analysis of *Lower bounds for incidences* uses two parallel
> lines to examine a lost sign in a specific geometric comparison. The report
> discusses a local alternative and the checks still needed. It does not
> establish that the paper's final incidence bounds fail.
>
> Inspect the source, argument and scope. Independent scientific review is
> pending:
> https://elvia-atlas.org/atlas/cases/math2025-0071.html

Possible visual: the two lines, their equal unsigned offsets and their distinct
phase-space positions. Bind labels and formulas to the inspected report. State
that this is an analytic construction rather than an experimental measurement.

### A proof repair that preserves the bound

> An intermediate inequality can fail while the final bound survives.
>
> ELVIA's preliminary analysis of *Private Hyperparameter Tuning with Ex-Post
> Guarantee* gives an exact example and proposes an alternative argument with
> the same constants. It does not demonstrate a privacy leak or refute the final
> privacy guarantee. Independent review of the analysis remains pending.
>
> Follow the argument:
> https://elvia-atlas.org/atlas/cases/neurips2025-0029-private-hyperparameter-tuning-with-ex-post-guarantee.html

Possible visual: a labeled number line distinguishing the disputed intermediate
bound, the exact expression and the lemma's final bound. Preserve the actual
parameters and hypotheses in the accompanying technical material.

## Selective illustration

Begin with three to five representative cases. Choose an example when a visual
clarifies a decisive comparison and the source, conditions and limits can be
explained accurately. A short table, a few equations or a plain paragraph may be
the better choice.

Every analysis should remain useful through its reasoning and evidence.
Custom illustration is optional; it is not a publication requirement for every
paper or finding. Expand the selected set when a new case adds a distinct
scientific lesson, rather than imposing a quota on the collection.

For each selected presentation, retain the original paper identity and identify
the inspected record revision. Check the current case before reusing this guide:
later source revisions or scientific decisions may change appropriate wording.
Link to the complete argument and keep material limitations visible. A diagram
does not independently confirm the analysis it explains.

## Claims and voice

Describe the mission confidently and the analysis specifically. Explain what a
reader can inspect, which step is at issue and what follows for reusing a result.
Keep the technical paper-auditing core: hypotheses, proofs, counterexamples,
source checks and the actual limits of evidence.

Distinguish a proposed repair from a verified repair, and both from author
adoption. Describe preliminary findings as preliminary. Do not convert assessment
counts into estimates of paper or venue error rates, present editorial selection
as scientific endorsement, or claim measured financial savings and downstream
benefits without evidence.

The broader mission may eventually inform research programs in many fields.
Today's descriptions should identify actual coverage and available services.
The project is maintained by Minhao Liu; do not imply that a staffed expert
panel, automatic paper-auditing service or established institutional network
already exists.

Media examples may occasionally explain a motivation. The main content remains
original scientific claims and their arguments. Public disagreement should make
the evidence easier to examine and leave room for substantive counterevidence.

## Publication workflow

Prepare one source-grounded analysis or reading path, then derive a short post
and a share image from the same material. Send readers directly to the relevant
analysis. Keep a reusable source record for the headline, diagram and scope.

External publication and outreach require the maintainer's actual channel and
task authorization. Repository documentation is not evidence that any post has
been sent. Track human reading and substantive feedback separately from crawler
traffic, and report observed results rather than promised reach.

# Concentrated guidance and square reading panels

Date: 6 November 2026. Status: local maintainer preview only.

The maintainer found the first preview too dense with small explanatory text
and repeated defensive declarations. The requested refinement concentrates
general guidance in Questions & answers and uses square background regions
to distinguish the important parts of each page.

The homepage keeps its mission, search, selected arguments and reading or
contribution actions. Repeated search explanations, pending-review declarations,
collection-boundary paragraphs and the repeated post-selection footer move
to linked FAQ disclosures. Duplicate audience/purpose/closing sections are
removed. A linked question opens on direct fragment navigation; account access
fragments retain their behavior.

Use the established ELVIA blue for the mission and a primary action region,
light blue for selected arguments or account entry, and a neutral panel for
directory controls or preparation. Keep square corners, strong typography and
white editing/reading surfaces. Legal and reading-guide sections share restrained
background regions; paper arguments remain complete and legible.

Cases keep their concise pending-review status and case-specific scientific
qualifications. Source version, analysis revision, origin and detailed status
remain in initial HTML in a named source-details disclosure. The introductory
argument and links remain visible. Repeated generic advice moves to the FAQ;
this does not remove counterevidence, surviving conclusions or source scope.

The contribution workspace keeps eligibility and required materials where users
need them. General account/publication/credit explanations link to the FAQ.
Authentication, selected-paper context, permission rules, report requirements,
scientific records, workflow and production state do not change.

This is an additional local review revision of decision 0016. No public rollout,
GitHub push, independent scientific review or new service capability is implied.

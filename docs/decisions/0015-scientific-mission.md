# Scientific mission and public identity

Date: 2026-11-06. Tracking: [Issue #36](https://github.com/chineseschrodingercat/elvia/issues/36).

## Decision

Retain **ELVIA** as the project name, **elvia** as the public repository name and
**elvia-atlas.org** as the website address. Use **Open Infrastructure for
Scientific Verification** as the public descriptor and **Build the future on
evidence.** as the mission line. **ELVIA Atlas** names the current public analysis
collection.

The public introduction will lead with the long-term ambition to make scientific
foundations open to inspection. It will then show the concrete present work:
source-bound AI-assisted analyses, inspectable arguments and counterevidence,
versioned records and a protocol for independent scientific review. The initial
collection's independent decisions remain pending.

The historical expansion, Expert-Led Verification of Inferences and Arguments,
can remain in existing background and historical material. This presentation
decision does not rename scientific records, package identifiers or citations.

## Scientific and communication boundaries

The technical core remains the inspection of scientific claims, conditions,
proofs, counterexamples and source-grounded evidence. Demonstrating a failure in
a specific claim does not establish failure of every conclusion in the paper.
Repairs are optional; their verification and author adoption retain separate
states. Findings that support the original reasoning or remain unresolved keep
their actual scope.

Ambition describes what the project seeks to build. It does not establish a
global reviewer network, automated audit service, measured savings, downstream
research impact or scientific confirmation. The collection is not a random
sample for estimating paper or venue error rates.

Custom visuals and flagship explanations will be selective. An initial group of
three to five cases is an editorial starting point, not a requirement for each
paper or finding. Every analysis remains supported by its complete reasoning,
sources and stated limits. Media-report fact-checking is not the project's
principal content direction.

## Delivery

The repository update covers its About description, README, reusable public
messaging and a concrete website design plan. The plan records proposed website
work; it does not deploy a redesign or change live accounts, scientific records,
assessments, authentication, hosting or operational state.

Use [the public messaging guide](../outreach.md) for reusable copy and
[the website design plan](../website-design-plan.md) for the next implementation.
Actual validation, integration and metadata readback belong to Issue #36.

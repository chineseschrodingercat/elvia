Project and methodology

Expert-Led Verification of Inferences and Arguments

Read an argument in context

Each record identifies a source version, a specific claim, the assumptions inspected, the reasoning and supporting evidence, and the scope of the conclusion. Supporting results, counterevidence and unresolved questions belong alongside the finding. A clarification is not a repair, and a preliminary assessment is not scientific confirmation.

The current Atlas contains 300 NeurIPS 2025 papers: 104 Major, 177 Minor, 3 Needs Evidence and 16 No Issue Found. These are bounded preliminary assessments of selected material. Independent scientific decisions remain pending. They are not whole-paper verdicts or estimates of venue error rates.

Scientific decisions, verification of a repair and author adoption are separate. Editorial acceptance of a contribution does not automatically establish any of them. Corrections to published scientific records use new immutable revisions, preserving their predecessors and source identity.

Governance and participation

Minhao Liu is ELVIA's founder and sole maintainer. Contributor applications are considered for relevant expertise and conflicts of interest. Named credit is optional. AI assistance must be described accurately; software checks cannot establish a person's authenticity, independence or scientific correctness.

Authors and readers can provide context, rebuttals and amendment requests. ELVIA does not represent an independent appeal panel or confidential scientific intake service as established. For project questions, contact contact@elvia-atlas.org.

Current service and next work

The Atlas, account access and contribution workspace are available. Future work includes gathering independent reviews, improving contribution workflows and reviewing evidence for formal publication. Public operation does not convert preliminary findings into confirmed results.

The website runs on the maintainer's computer and can be unavailable while it is off, asleep or disconnected. Source development is maintained on GitHub. Each case provides a source citation, a readable report and the underlying evidence.

Cite the evidence you used

Credit Minhao Liu and ELVIA, link to elvia-atlas.org, and identify the case ID, record revision, source version and access date. Cite the original paper separately. Download a case's original article and report PDFs for reading. Versioned source records preserve the full evidence and source identity. If you modify ELVIA's scientific material, identify your changes.

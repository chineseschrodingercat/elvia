Contributing context about your own paper

Keep the argument
within reach.

Working with a paper often means working through a question: does this assumption hold in my setting? How does this step follow? Would a small example make the result easier to use?

ELVIA keeps that follow-up work close to the original research. A clear explanation, a confirmed argument or a correction can all help the next person read, apply or extend a result.

For readers

Find the original passage, follow the steps and see which assumptions matter for your use of a result.

For authors

Help readers understand your work as you intended. A relevant assumption, an explanation or an updated version can give a note the context it needs.

For reviewers

Choose one question in your area. Explain what you checked and what you learned, including when the original argument holds up.

A note you can follow.

Each note keeps the source passage, the argument and its limits together. It states what was checked and what remains open. When the interpretation changes, earlier versions remain available.

Before you use
an analysis.

Have the current notes been independently reviewed?

The current collection contains preliminary AI analyses. Independent scientific review remains pending. A record's evidence, reasoning and stated limits are available for inspection.

What makes a useful review?

A useful review identifies the passage examined, explains the reasoning and states its limits. It may support the original argument, add a clarification, identify a possible issue or leave the question unresolved.

Can I contribute to a note about my own paper?

Yes. An explanation, a source correction or an updated version may help readers. Your response should remain identifiable as an author contribution. Independent review is a separate role. The local contribution workspace currently accepts independent analyses and reviews. A separate author-response intake is still planned.

Do I need to review an entire paper?

A contribution can focus on one claim, source reference or software task. Describe what you examined and what remains outside your scope.

Can I download and cite the evidence?

Records provide downloadable evidence and source information. Cite the case and revision you used, together with the original research. The repository includes software citation guidance and material-specific licenses.

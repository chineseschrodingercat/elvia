About the project and its method

Questions & answers.

What is the review status of these analyses?

The current collection contains preliminary AI analyses. Independent scientific review remains pending. An assessment describes the inspected claims and their recorded impact, rather than a verdict on an entire paper. Each case keeps its source version, argument, counterevidence and limits. Read the review method.

What does the search look through?

Search matches paper titles, authors, identifiers and public analysis text, including retained arguments. It does not generate answers or perform a new paper review. Try conformal coverage or Lower bounds for incidences. Open the matching problem to examine the result and its scope.

Which papers are in the Atlas?

The current collection contains 514 Math 2025 articles, 400 ICML 2025 papers and 300 NeurIPS 2025 papers: 1,214 versioned records. It is a selected collection, not a complete map of research or an estimate of paper error rates. Its published records are read-only; accepted contributions enter a separate publication workflow.

Do I need an account or a whole-paper review?

Reading is open without an account. A contribution can focus on one claim, source reference or software check. Register with an academic or approved research-institution email when you are ready to save work. Named public credit requires accepted, non-practice work and your explicit consent; project acknowledgement does not confer a journal appointment, accreditation or automatic ORCID recognition. A clarification, a verified step, counterevidence or support for the original argument can all help. See a small contribution example or preview the PDF and LaTeX templates.

What happens after I submit a report?

Submissions are reviewed before publication. Accepted work receives a versioned contribution record; named credit is optional. Editorial acceptance, an independent scientific decision, repair verification and author adoption are separate. A new analysis does not silently replace the source record. Read the contribution guide.

Can I respond to an analysis of my own paper?

An explanation, source correction, updated version or rebuttal may help readers. It should remain identifiable as an author contribution; independent review is a separate role. The workspace currently accepts independent analyses and reviews. A separate author-response intake is still planned. Contact the maintainer about the current route.

How should I use a reported finding?

Read the exact source statement, check the argument under its hypotheses and keep the case-specific scope in view. A local counterexample or implementation discrepancy does not by itself establish a failure of every result, benchmark or application. Those scientific limits remain beside the relevant case; a summary does not establish broader effects.

Can I download and cite the evidence?

Cases provide source information, citations and public evidence. Cite the case and revision you used together with the original research. Published reader bundles include the selected report files. Citation and licensing details.

Contact ELVIA.

Questions, feedback or help getting started — we would like to hear from you.

Get in touch

contact@elvia-atlas.org

Your message reaches Minhao Liu, ELVIA’s founder and sole maintainer.

For account help, include the email you registered with and a brief description of the issue. For a question about an analysis, include the page link and the relevant finding.

To share a scientific report or revision, use the contribution workspace so that its evidence and history stay together. Software issues and suggestions can also be recorded in the public project issue tracker.

Please keep passwords and verification codes out of your message.

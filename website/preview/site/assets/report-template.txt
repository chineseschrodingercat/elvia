HOW TO PACKAGE
Save your edited report as report.txt. Compress that file, plus any needed
verification files, into one ZIP. No special folders or manifest are required.
Windows: right-click the file(s), then Compress to ZIP file.
macOS: right-click the file(s), then Compress.
Use a standard, unencrypted ZIP, at most 10 MB. Do not include credentials or
unrelated private files. A saved draft can be incomplete; submission also needs
the paper identity, required source materials and your own declarations.

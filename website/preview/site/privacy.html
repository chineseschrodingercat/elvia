Privacy notice

ELVIA · Updated October 22, 2026

Who runs this workspace

Minhao Liu maintains ELVIA at elvia-atlas.org. The website runs on the maintainer’s computer through Cloudflare. It is unavailable when that computer is off, asleep or disconnected.

Information we store

The local service stores your name, account email, password hash, verified-email timestamp, application details, drafts, submitted versions, attachments, decisions and activity history. Passwords use salted hashes. Verification codes are stored as keyed hashes, expire after ten minutes and allow at most five attempts. Short-lived email challenge records and daily usage counters support abuse prevention. Exact-email role reservations, access-status changes and management actions are stored locally. Suspension ends existing sessions and preserves contribution history.

Reviewer applications may include position, affiliation, expertise and a profile URL. These are available to authorized admins and maintainers. Your account email is not published in the contributor directory.

Who can see submitted work

Contributor drafts and packages are available to their owner and authorized admins and maintainers. Each maintainer’s unsubmitted drafts are private to that maintainer. Account management follows role boundaries: admins manage contributors; maintainers also manage admins. Maintainers cannot manage one another’s accounts. An assigned reviewer can access the materials needed for that review. A return link is not a public access token: sign-in and permission checks still apply. Accepted task summaries and maintainer final notes may be read without an account. Private uploads and account details remain restricted. Named contribution credit is optional and is shown only after acceptance and your per-contribution choice. Acceptance does not certify scientific correctness.

Email and Google services

When you request a verification code or password recovery, the configured email provider receives your recipient address and the message for delivery. Provider retention and policies also apply. Password recovery uses an existing verified account email or an admin or maintainer's already-linked Google email. A recovery link expires after fifteen minutes and works once; the local database stores its keyed digest and request metadata, rather than its raw token. Completing recovery invalidates existing local sessions and pending authentication requests while retaining account roles, Google links and any suspension. The service does not send marketing mail.

Google sign-in is available only to existing admins and maintainers after configuration and explicit account linking. Google may provide the account holder’s name, verified email and stable account identifier. The linked verified email can also be used for that staff account's local password recovery. We request only basic identity scopes, not Gmail, Drive or contacts access. Google access and ID tokens are used to complete sign-in and are not retained. Your Google password is never received by ELVIA. Google’s own privacy policy applies to its sign-in service.

Website delivery and security

Cloudflare delivers the website and processes request metadata, including network addresses. Turnstile checks protect sign-in and account-email requests from automated abuse. Cloudflare receives the information needed to perform those checks. ELVIA stores keyed network identifiers for short-lived abuse limits; it does not publish them. Cloudflare’s own privacy policy applies.

Other external requests and cookies

Eligibility checks request public paper metadata and journal metrics from the relevant bibliographic service or publisher. The requested paper identifier or public URL and the host’s network address may be visible to that provider. Private report ZIPs, source uploads, passwords and reviewer applications are not included. No model service is called. Fonts and interface assets are local; there are no analytics or advertising trackers.

Essential HTTP-only cookies maintain a local sign-in for up to seven days and a Google sign-in request for up to ten minutes. They are not advertising cookies. Signing out invalidates the current session; changing the local password invalidates other sessions.

Retention and requests

Account and contribution records remain on the host until the maintainer removes them. Submitted versions and decisions are retained to preserve the history of a contribution. Local backups can retain older copies. Ask the maintainer to review an access, correction, export or deletion request and explain any record that must remain for contribution history. ELVIA does not provide automatic account deletion or promise a fixed deletion period.

Email contact@elvia-atlas.org or contact the project maintainer, Minhao Liu, for account or privacy questions. Please do not post passwords, verification codes or private packages in public issues.

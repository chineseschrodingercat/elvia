What can I contribute?

ELVIA accepts focused scientific analyses and reviews. A contribution can verify a derivation, explain a missing condition, construct a worked example, examine a reproducibility question or provide counterevidence to a proposed concern. A result that supports the original paper is useful too.

You can start with a claim in the NeurIPS 2025 evidence collection or submit another paper with your own analysis. The current Atlas contains 300 paper records; its initial analyses await independent scientific review.

Can a PhD student participate?

Yes. Start with a question within your expertise; you do not need to review every part of a paper. Register with an academic or approved research-institution email and verify access to that mailbox. A verified account can submit its own paper analysis. Reviewing another contributor's work requires a one-time application describing your background, relevant expertise and conflicts of interest.

A student working through a proof can contribute a precise mathematical check. An experimental researcher may add context about assumptions or measurements. A research software contributor can help maintain the open-source project through GitHub's contribution guide.

From a question to a useful report

  1. Identify the source. Record the paper version and exact section, equation, figure or code location.
  2. State the question. Say what you checked and the assumptions under which your conclusion applies.
  3. Show the reasoning. Include the derivation, calculation, worked example or other evidence. Describe relevant counterevidence and unresolved steps.
  4. Submit your report. Use the contribution workspace to attach your report and supporting files. Describe any AI assistance accurately.
  5. Respond to review. The maintainer checks submissions before publication. Accepted changes become a new version with the earlier record preserved.

Credit, revisions and independence

Accepted work receives a versioned contribution record; named public credit is optional. An editorial decision to publish a contribution is separate from scientific confirmation, repair verification and adoption by the paper's authors. See the review protocol for those distinctions.

If you are an author or have a connection to the paper, disclose that context. Author input can clarify a source or change the interpretation, while independent review remains a separate role. Questions about author responses can go to contact@elvia-atlas.org.

Looking for a different kind of contribution?

You might want to discuss a publication, participate in a venue's review process or contribute to reproducibility guidance. Our guide to research review and verification platforms explains these different uses.

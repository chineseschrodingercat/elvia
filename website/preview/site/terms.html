Website terms

ELVIA · Effective October 22, 2026

Purpose and availability

This workspace supports focused examination of research arguments, source context and revisions. ELVIA is an early community project, not a journal, official conference review service or institutional certification. Access and features may change. The website connects the 300-paper Atlas with a contribution workspace and is unavailable when the maintainer’s computer is offline.

Accounts and eligibility

Contributor registration requires control of an academic email or an approved research-institution email. Verification confirms mailbox access, not academic expertise, employment or identity beyond that mailbox. No endorsement is required. Use accurate account and application details and keep your password and codes private.

A verified account can submit its own analysis without a reviewer application. Reviewing another contribution requires a one-time approved application. New paper submissions are limited to two per calendar day; approved reviewers may claim one task and submit one review or amendment per day. The day follows America/Chicago. Saving a draft does not use a submission allowance. Admins and maintainers are exempt from daily contribution limits and the reviewer application. Admins can review others’ submissions and add ordinary revisions. Maintainers also manage admins and retain final-decision authority. Website roles do not grant GitHub or host-system access.

Submissions and responsible participation

Submit material you have permission to share for review. Include the source, scope, reasoning, checks and limitations. Disclose substantive AI assistance and the actual model configuration; manual work is welcome. Address claims and evidence respectfully, disclose relevant conflicts and avoid personal allegations or unrelated personal information.

By submitting, you permit ELVIA to store the work, retain submitted versions and make it available to authorized admins, maintainers and an assigned reviewer. Accepted task summaries and maintainer final notes can be publicly read. Uploading a private package does not itself publish that package. Material-specific licensing and release of private attachments require separate review. Do not upload credentials, confidential third-party information or material whose sharing is restricted.

Collection limits and decisions

The published scope rules determine intake. Chemistry and biological science require either a most recent verifiable dated JIF greater than 10 or inclusion in the 2026 TOP journal list (major-category Zone 1 and Top). Either route qualifies; paper identity must still be verified. Computer science uses the listed CCF A full/main-conference scope; mathematics uses the expanded project journal list. Verified official arXiv preprints are eligible in all fields without venue thresholds and remain labeled as preprints.

Automated checks address metadata and scope, not scientific correctness. An admin or maintainer may request clarification or decline a submission. Admins cannot decide on their own submissions. Only maintainers can issue a final project decision. Contributors can request an amendment; prior versions remain in the record.

Credit and interpretation

Accepted contributions receive a versioned record. Named contributor listing is optional and does not imply employment, authorship of a future paper, an academic credential or endorsement by an institution. A certificate or contribution record acknowledges the work described, not a general qualification.

Read the original research and each analysis’s limits before relying on it. Notes may be incomplete or revised. ELVIA does not guarantee the accuracy or completeness of a scientific conclusion.

Administration and changes

An admin may restrict contributor access for misuse, false account information, harmful uploads or attempts to bypass limits. A maintainer may also restrict admin access. Each maintainer controls their own account; peer maintainers cannot suspend or downgrade it. These terms and the privacy notice may be revised as the service changes. Questions can be directed to Minhao Liu. Account and privacy requests can also be sent to contact@elvia-atlas.org.

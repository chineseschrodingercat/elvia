Read each finding in context

A check may find a candidate issue, clarify a condition, reproduce a known issue or find no issue in its scope. Independent scientific decisions, repair verification and author adoption are separate later steps.

Assessment definitions and collection counts
Major
An established error undermines a core claim, or a substantial reasoning or derivation step is clearly invalid.
Minor
A supported local defect without demonstrated damage to the paper's central conclusions.
Needs Evidence
The available evidence does not settle the concern.
No Issue Found
No error established within the inspected scope.

Keep the argument
within reach.

Expert-Led Verification of Inferences and Arguments is a long-term effort to make scientific reasoning easier to examine and reuse across mathematics, machine learning, computer science and bioinformatics.

The current toolkit preserves evidence. Qualified independent review and confidential scientific intake are future work.

  • Start with a precise claim

    Record the source version, assumptions and exact proposition being checked.

  • Keep the working

    Preserve original checks, counterevidence, failed attempts and limits alongside the analysis.

  • Record decisions separately

    A computation does not establish expert agreement, a verified repair or adoption by the authors.
